# Layer map

Every component names its justification: provable in Lean now, provable with analysis, checked per certificate, tested in CI, open mathematics, or a declared value. Thirteen components can be proved in Lean now and four more with analysis; a fifth, the Jacobi frontier defect, is out of scope until it's well-posed. The other eight are checked per instance, tested, open, or declared. Layers sort components by what justifies them, not by what enforces them: a Norm row is a declared value, and whether a gate also enforces it is a separate question. In v1 no gate enforces the Norm rows ([decisions/RAILS.md](decisions/RAILS.md)). Every row has a status and a pointer. A Proved row names its Lean theorems and its limits, and points to the proof file and the milestone note that explains each proof. Every other row says why it is out of scope, or not in v1, and points to that reason: the CI layer, a certificate, open math or the Norm layer. The Checked by column says what in this repository checks each row today, and says so when nothing does.

## The statements, and what each version adds

Since Phase 2 the canonical text is [`lean/JSG/Defs.lean`](../lean/JSG/Defs.lean) (definitions) and [`lean/JSG/Statements.lean`](../lean/JSG/Statements.lean) (theorems), which compile against Mathlib v4.34.1. Read them there: the master guide's copy of them, its Lean 4 statement stubs, is gone. Every theorem carries a `Mutant:` comment naming a plausible wrong implementation that fails it; a theorem without one doesn't earn its place. `lean/JSG/Tests/Mutants.lean` proves each mutant's counterexample and a witness instance. Confirm every Mathlib name with Loogle before relying on it.

`statements-v2` adds typed obligations with witness types and authorities (pass-soundness), with a ticket for each of a window's stages; leases; a concrete Loader whose invariant is L1–L10; and residual threading through any number of stages. Three pieces remain deferred: receipt integrity (replaying the content at a receipt's address yields its grade) and the token-bucket trace invariant for weakenings, both to a later version ([QUEUE.md](QUEUE.md), Q6), and a `Noninterfering` proof for each concrete grader. v1's `monotone_compose` and `discharge_wf` are dropped, since each held for any implementation. `no_flip` and `mustFail_monotone` are restated where they can fail: on the grader, and on the CI gate.

`statements-v3` adds the analysis layer's other three statements: CVaR tail to level, the local KL–Fisher bound in whitened coordinates, and the DRO envelope identity for any dual of the form ηρ + g(η), with η the multiplier. Each source claim needed correcting first ([STATEMENTS_REVIEW.md](STATEMENTS_REVIEW.md#statements-v3)). Strong duality, that the dual equals the worst-case risk, and the Jacobi frontier defect wait ([QUEUE.md](QUEUE.md), Q8 and Q10).

`statements-v4` adds executable twins of the core's graded values, `combine`, `verdict`, residual threading, widths and leases. They compute in `WithTop ℚ≥0`, and each has a theorem that it agrees with its frozen definition ([EXECUTABLE_GATE.md](EXECUTABLE_GATE.md) §1). The obligation and Loader twins wait for a version with an implementation to test them against ([QUEUE.md](QUEUE.md), Q11).

`statements-v5` adds four statements about the ordinal core, the part of the model the pilot uses ([ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/marleymcwilliams/LibSpec/blob/statements-v5/guide/ROADMAP.md#milestone-5-the-cores-gap-statements), Milestone 5). `accept_bounds_truth` says what an accept means: if two stages' grades enclose their true values, and those values compose no worse than `combine` assumes, an accepted combination bounds the true composed value by θ. `combine_respects_narrowing` and `narrowing_keeps_accept` say that more evidence about a stage only narrows what it combines into, and never turns an acceptance into a rejection. `definitelyFails_spec` tells a definite rejection, with some axis's lower end above its threshold, from one that more evidence can undo. [Milestone 5](https://github.com/marleymcwilliams/LibSpec/blob/3b481d5e951bb30300195598e9dada5a753ab61c/guide/MILESTONE_5.md) proved them, and their form is [QUEUE.md](QUEUE.md), Q16.

`statements-v6` adds `narrows_unknown`: every grade narrows the grade that is unknown on every axis, which pins the unknown to [0, ∞], and its twin too, through `xunknown_agrees`. It's the gamers' candidate 1, proved in the same PR ([STATEMENTS_REVIEW.md](STATEMENTS_REVIEW.md#statements-v6); [QUEUE.md](QUEUE.md), Q17).
