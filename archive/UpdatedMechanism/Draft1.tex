\begin{abstract}
\noindent \textbf{Trust through verifiability.} This draft proposes a research--neutral, safety--first architecture for verifiable computation. Every step produces a typed, canonical receipt; aggregates form a header--only proof surface that can be checked from bytes. Determinism is ensured via canonical encodings and domain--separated hashing; governance is monotone and mechanized; control is formulated as distributionally robust optimization (DRO) with first--order optimality receipts and cache--admitted second--order stability certificates. Receipts support selective disclosure without breaking chain integrity. The aim is accessibility without compromising rigor.
\end{abstract}
\section{Objectives and Positioning}
\noindent We formalize an auditable, \emph{verifiable} notion of trust without presupposing any downstream domain. Core objectives: (i) compositional evidence with byte--level verification, (ii) determinism by design, (iii) mechanized monotone governance, (iv) transparent control with explicit certification, and (v) selective disclosure with intact chain integrity.
\section{Acceptance Algebra and Aggregation}
Let $Q=\prod_i Q_i$ be acceptance coordinates with preorder (larger=worse). Split coordinates into max--channels and sum--channels; define aggregation $\wedge$ as coordinatewise $\max$ on max--channels and $+$ on sum--channels, with fail--closed absorbers for unknowns.
\begin{definition}[Adapters and Unknowns]
Adapters $g:\mathrm{Raw}\to Q$ are monotone homomorphisms satisfying $g(r\oplus r')=g(r)\wedge g(r')$, $g(\varnothing)=\top_{\mathrm{unk}}$. Sum--coordinates may use capped unknowns $\top_{\mathrm{unk}}^{(\beta)}$ under window caps with run--scope ledgers.
\end{definition}
\section{Receipts, Proof Surface, and Selective Disclosure}
Headers (schema id, predicate id, verdict, prev\_digest, payload\_root, env lock, hash suite) are encoded in deterministic CBOR; digests use domain--separated hashing. The proof surface is the Merkle root over canonical headers.
\begin{definition}[Selective Disclosure]
Receipts commit to payload Merkle roots; a reveal discloses only requested fields with inclusion proofs. Chain integrity uses header digests, so redaction does not alter the proof surface.
\end{definition}

\textbf{Audit Hook.} Any single--byte mutation in a header flips the Merkle root; payload redactions keep the root unchanged when proofs verify against the committed payload root.
\end{auditbox}
\section{Norms: STL and the SMT Gate}
Norms are expressed in Signal Temporal Logic (STL) with quantitative semantics; bounded monitors unroll to SMT--LIB (QF\_LRA) for CI gating. Dense--time semantics are approximated via robust--satisfaction intervals; the lower bound is enforced.
\section{Control as Distributionally Robust Optimization}
We cast inverse control as a distributionally robust program over a Wasserstein ball. Runtime receipts log (i) ambiguity radii and convex class, (ii) KKT residuals and dual gaps (first--order), (iii) optional positive--real / input--to--state stability admission (second--order) from a revocation--checked cache.
\section{Mechanized Governance (Monotone Safety)}
The must--fail set in the active norms bundle is monotone under versioning; deltas are checked mechanically in CI. Optional entailment between STL versions may be discharged via SMT. Epoch summaries periodically Merkle--commit prior receipts for archival without breaking verification.
\section{Ethics Rails and Clamp States}
Rails provide banded operating regions (Green/Amber/Red) with hysteresis and dwell; sentinel thresholds and mirror gaps enforce conservative transitions. Policies are expressed as typed receipts with selective disclosure.

\section{Trust Scoring (Transparent, Calibrated)}
A transparent trust score aggregates evaluator utility, robustness, anomaly EWMA, safety margins, certificate status, and clamp state; it is cached and periodically calibrated (e.g., isotonic regression) against independent audits. Stale inputs widen intervals and raise uncertainty flags.
\section{Threats and Mitigations (At a Glance)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Supply chain / environment drift: deterministic builds and environment locks by digest.
  \item Ledger tampering: header--only proof surface with domain--separated hashing.
  \item Signature malleability: normative COSE profile over deterministic CBOR.
  \item Denial via growth: batching, epoch summaries, rate limits in the norms bundle.
  \item Privacy: selective disclosure with minimal proofs; headers suffice for chain checks.
\end{itemize}
\section{Evaluation Program (Safety Before Scale)}
E1 Determinism across platforms (equal roots). E2 Tamper detection (bit flips change root). E3 First--order certification efficacy (violations detected). E4 Governance monotonicity (CI blocks regressions). E5 Calibration validity (predictive trust vs.\ audits).
\section{Conclusion}
Verifiable behavior is treated as a computable property: formal norms, deterministic execution, compositional receipts, and transparent control close the loop, enabling byte--level audit without dependence on narrative claims.
