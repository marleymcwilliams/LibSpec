\begin{abstract}
Part~III converts the Joint Strategic Geometry program into a \emph{proof-carrying governor}. Every runtime claim about coupling, range, and safety is falsifiable via logged witnesses: (i) KKT traces and dual gaps for soft-transport DRO; (ii) GKYP/LMI feasibility certificates (band endpoints, margin $\constref{PRmargin}$, uncertainty set, dual multipliers) for PR; (iii) $\omega$-automata accepting/rejecting runs for LTL specs; and (iv) a hash-chained Provenance Ladder. Two technical closures make the geometry operational: a \emph{saltation--defect} bound parameterized by $(\constref{kappamax},\constref{thetamin},\constref{rtube})$ that `spends' the frontier-defect budget inside the ISS supply rate and sharpens the Decision-Range Conservativity bound; and a \emph{holonomy budget} that limits rotational mass via Davis--Kahan/Wedin plane-drift across crossings.

Sensing is windowed and \emph{identifiable}: Effective Coupling Space $E_t$ is certified by mixed cumulants with FDR control, then stress-tested by \emph{instruments/interventions} on $\Gamma$ or $G$. A \emph{Minimum Transfer Condition (MTC)} promotes a discovery only if it (a) reduces held-out transport error, (b) passes a calibrated SPRT on $M_t$, and (c) preserves Fisher conditioning. The KL--Fisher neighborhood is made \emph{operational} by $r=\constref{EFeps}/\constref{Lpsi}$ with an automatic \emph{leakage clamp} driven by $\constref{chi}(t)$ that shrinks $r$ and freezes new discoveries when conditioning degrades.

Control is a CLF/CBF-QP projected by $\Pi$ with frontier-aware CBF invariance and PR certification on a band; Decision Range $R_t$ is the reported admissible-control volume. A normative dictionary maps legal/fiscal levers (TRO/PI, SLA, anti-commandeering, etc.) to concrete controller contracts. We ship a four-tier artifact (Transport Pack, Provenance Ladder, Coupling ID Sheet with MTC badges, Automata Evidence) and a benchmark suite (negative control, degeneracy stress, formal-spec acceptance). The result is a governor that measurably reduces coupling mass $M_t$, increases decision range $R_t$ subject to I$_5$, and remains auditable end-to-end.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} information geometry; Fisher metric; mixed cumulants; DRO; PR control; CBF/CLF-QP; formal methods; $\omega$-automata; provenance; benchmarks.

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\paragraph{Aim.}
Part~III (\emph{JSGIII}) turns the Joint Strategic Geometry program into a \emph{proof-carrying governor}: every runtime decision is fenced by guards consumed in a fixed order and backed by replayable evidence. The governor is designed to be falsifiable at window granularity and portable across deployments via container pins, solver transcripts, and tiered artifacts.

\paragraph{What is governed (I\textsubscript{1}–I\textsubscript{5}).}
We re-state the five invariants that all runtime logic must maintain; each is enforced by specific guards and acceptance tests:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{I\(_1\) Hybrid safety (frontiers).} Tube-aware invariance at crossings, No-Zeno dwell, and a numeric saltation audit; failures deterministically reject the run. \uses{Rule~\ref{rule:frontier-freeze}; Rule~\ref{rule:grazing}; Lem.~\ref{lem:cbf-salt}; \jsgiLem{\lblNoZeno}.}
\item \textbf{I\(_2\) Statistical locality (EF).} All Fisher–quadratic uses lie inside a certified EF neighborhood of radius \(r=\constref{EFeps}/\constref{Lpsi}\), guarded and logged each window. \uses{Thm.~\ref{thm:FIMbound}; Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}.}
\item \textbf{I\(_3\) Identifiability-first discovery.} Promotions pass MTC gates with DRO envelope precondition and clamp=Green; CIS rows are complete. \uses{Rule~\ref{rule:promotion}; Rule~\ref{rule:dvdrho}; Prop.~\ref{prop:kkt}; \jsgiiDef{\lblMTC}.}
\item \textbf{I\(_4\) Actuation-basis stability.} Plane drift is budgeted by holonomy/Harmonic Rotational Mass; rising degeneracy blocks basis updates or triggers \(k\)-escalation/switch-dwell. \uses{Def.~\ref{def:hol-harm}; Lem.~\ref{lem:usc-planes}; Lem.~\ref{lem:switching}.}
\item \textbf{I\(_5\) Rights-plane \& PR stability.} Band-limited PR witnesses gate control updates; rights-plane tails pass CVaR checks; decision range is conservative under degraded guards. \uses{Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}; Rem.~\ref{rem:cvar}; Prop.~\ref{prop:cons}.}
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Three pillars and two closures.}
JSGIII fuses: (i) \emph{geometric/control} contracts (CLF/CBF-QP with tube-level saltation and band-limited PR via GKYP); (ii) \emph{information-theoretic} guards (EF/KL radius and a DRO envelope witness that preconditions sequential decisions); and (iii) \emph{formal/operational} evidence (ω-automata over KPI row-hashes and a hash-chained Provenance Ladder). Two closures make the geometry operational at run time: a \emph{priced frontier defect} that prints into the ISS budget, and a \emph{holonomy budget} that limits rotational mass across crossings. \uses{\secref{sec:closures-v2}; \jsgiThm{\lblISS}.}

\section{Operational Contracts and Evidence (v2)}\label{sec:ops-v2}

\paragraph{Scope.}

\subsection{(a) Geometric diagnosis: DEC/Hodge, holonomy, dwell, Fisher continuity}\label{subsec:ops-geom}
\emph{\layertag{Rep/Alg/Inf/Ctrl}}

\begin{rulex}{Frontier projection freeze (Δ)}\label{rule:frontier-freeze}
When inside any frontier tube of radius $\constref{rtube}$, \emph{freeze} basis updates and project control to the interior half-space aligned with the certified normal. Concretely: (i) hold the actuation plane/basis fixed; (ii) zero all promotions; (iii) project $u$ by $u\leftarrow \Pi_{\mathrm{safe}}(u)$ using the previous off-tube basis; (iv) log \texttt{freeze\_start/end} and the tube id; (v) include \texttt{cbf\_audit\_id} in the window proof print. Unfreeze only after a completed dwell interval $\ge\constref{taumin}$ with positive normal speed CI.
\uses{(C1) $\constref{rtube}$, (C3) $\constref{taumin}$.}
\end{rulex}

\begin{rulex}{Grazing-aware dwell escalation (Δ)}\label{rule:grazing}

\subsection{Frontier calculus: No-Zeno and geometry-scaled defect}\label{subsec:frontier-closure}
\emph{\layertag{Geom/Alg/Ctrl}}

\begin{lemma}[No-Zeno dwell and crossing bound]\label{lem:nozeno-impl}
If the closed-loop vector field satisfies a one-sided Lipschitz condition with constant $\constref{LF}$ and frontier crossings are strictly transversal (principal-angle separation $\ge \constref{thetamin}$), then the minimum dwell $\constref{taumin}>0$ exists and
\[
N_{\mathrm{cross}}(0,t)\ \le\ \frac{t}{\constref{taumin}}.
\]
\uses{\jsgiLem{\lblNoZeno}; (C1) $\constref{thetamin}$; (C3) $\constref{LF},\constref{taumin}$.}
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Jacobi-defect budget with geometric pricing]\label{lem:jacobi-upgraded}
Let $C_{\mathrm{fr}}=C_{\mathrm{fr}}(\constref{kappamax},\constref{thetamin},\constref{rtube},\constref{Smax},\constref{Gmax})$ denote the per-crossing constant from the frontier calculus. Then
\[
\int_0^T\!\|\Delta_{\mathrm{fr}}\|\,\mathrm dt\ \le\ C_{\mathrm{fr}}\ N_{\mathrm{cross}}(0,T)
\ \le\ C_{\mathrm{fr}}\ \frac{T}{\constref{taumin}}.
\]
\uses{\jsgiLem{\lblJacobiDefect}; \jsgiLem{\lblNoZeno}; (C1) $\constref{kappamax},\constref{thetamin},\constref{rtube}$; (C2) $\constref{Smax},\constref{Gmax}$; (C3) $\constref{taumin}$.}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proposition}[ISS budget printout with frontier spend]\label{prop:iss-spend}
Under band-limited PR control and the frontier conditions above, the windowed storage $H$ obeys
\[
H(t)\ \le\ \beta(H(0))\ +\ \int_0^t \gamma(\|w(\tau)\|)\,\mathrm d\tau\ +\ C_{\mathrm{fr}}\,N_{\mathrm{cross}}(0,t),
\]
and hence $H(t)\le \beta(\cdot)+\int\gamma(\cdot)+ (C_{\mathrm{fr}}/\constref{taumin})\,t$.
\uses{\jsgiThm{\lblISS}; Lem.~\ref{lem:jacobi-upgraded}; (C6) PR margin $\constref{epsilon}$.}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Conservativity link (Δ)]\label{prop:cons-link}
If the clamp is not Green or if the PR margin crowds $\constsymPRmargin$, enforce conservative range policy $\hat R_t$ non-increase. With the ledger from Lem.~\ref{lem:jacobi-upgraded},
\[
\hat R_{t+\tau}-\hat R_t\ \le\ \underbrace{\Gamma(\text{guard degradation})}_{\le 0}\ +\ \underbrace{\Xi\big(C_{\mathrm{fr}}\,N_{\mathrm{cross}}(t,t+\tau)\big)}_{\le 0},
\]
so any positive drift flags a policy violation.
\uses{Prop.~\ref{prop:iss-spend}; \secref{subsec:range}; Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}.}
\end{proposition}

\begin{acceptchecks}
\item \textbf{No-Zeno:} window report prints $\constref{taumin}$ and verifies $N_{\mathrm{cross}}\le t/\constref{taumin}$ using logged crossing times (\jsgiLem{\lblNoZeno}).
\item \textbf{Defect ledger:} prints both $C_{\mathrm{fr}} N_{\mathrm{cross}}$ and $(C_{\mathrm{fr}}/\constref{taumin})t$; values must match within numerical tolerance (\jsgiLem{\lblJacobiDefect}).
\item \textbf{Proof print presence (Δ):} the window’s proof print includes \{\texttt{pr\_witness\_id}, \texttt{cbf\_audit\_id}\} in EF$\rightarrow$DRO$\rightarrow$PR$\rightarrow$CBF$\rightarrow\omega$ order.
\end{acceptchecks}

\section{Sensing, Whitening, and Identification (v2)}\label{sec:sense-v2}

\paragraph{Goal.}
Turn raw telemetry into \emph{auditable} evidence about coupling and safety: windowing $\to$ Fisher/whitening $\to$ mixed cumulants $\to$ leakage/clamp state $\to$ promotion gates (MTC$>$correlation) guarded by the DRO envelope check. All outputs feed Tier-1…4 artifacts and the KPI panel; each window emits a \emph{proof print} with stable IDs in the guard order EF$\rightarrow$DRO$\rightarrow$PR$\rightarrow$CBF$\rightarrow\omega$ (cf. \secref{subsec:frontier-closure}).

\subsection{Windowing, Fisher objects, and whitening}\label{subsec:window-fim}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch/Inf/Comp}}

Let a rolling window of length $\constref{tau}$ produce telemetry $Z_t=(X_t,Y_t,U_t)$ with features $\phi_t$. Compute the window Fisher object,
\[
F_t \equiv \constref{F}\ :=\ \mathbb{E}_t\!\big[\nabla_\theta \ell(\theta;Z)\,\nabla_\theta \ell(\theta;Z)^\top\big],
\]
and any numerically stable whitener $W_t$ with $W_t^\top W_t=F_t$. Define \emph{whitened} features and the windowed cumulant vector by
\[
\tilde\phi_t:=W_t\,\phi_t,\qquad
\kappa_t := \text{vec of selected mixed cumulants of }(\tilde\phi_t,Y_t,U_t)\,.
\]
The \emph{coupling mass} is $M_t=\kappa_t^\top F_t^{-1}\kappa_t$ (Def.~\ref{def:coupling-mass}). The EF radius uses \emph{EF}~tolerance $\varepsilon$ (distinct from PR margins): $r=\varepsilon/\constref{Lpsi}$ (Thm.~\ref{thm:FIMbound}).

\uses{(C4) $\constref{tau}$; (C5) $\constref{F},\constref{Lpsi}$; (C10) $\varepsilon$.}

\begin{remark}[Whitening audit \& hashes]\label{rem:white-hash}
Tier-1 must include \texttt{FIM\_hash} and \texttt{whitening\_hash}; parity jobs re-derive $(F_t,W_t)$ and require bit-match within tolerance. A stable \texttt{ef\_guard\_id} (below) binds any Fisher-quadratic usage to a specific guard verdict. 
\uses{\jsgiiSec{\lblSchemas}; Def.~\ref{def:tierpacks}.}
\end{remark}

\subsection{Coverage policy and variance for $M_t$}\label{subsec:coverage}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch/Comp}}

Report $M_t$ with dependence-aware uncertainty. For smooth functionals, compute HAC (Newey–West) CIs; for non-smooth panels or stress tests, use block bootstrap (MBB/CBB). Publish a \emph{coverage panel} per window:
\[
\texttt{coverage}=\{\texttt{method}\in\{\text{HAC},\text{MBB},\text{CBB}\},\ \texttt{bandwidth/blocklen},\ \texttt{CI}\}.
\]
Bandwidth/block length are \emph{pre-registered} selectors (Defaults \& Knobs) learned via pilot ACF decay and logged as part of Tier-1. CI render \emph{fails} if any selector is missing.

\uses{(C5) $F$; KPI panel in \secref{subsec:ops-kpis}.}

\begin{lemma}[Consistent uncertainty for $M_t$]\label{lem:Mt-ci}
Under standard mixing and moment conditions on $\{\kappa_t\}$, HAC variance of $M_t$ exists and yields asymptotically valid Wald intervals; MBB/CBB give finite-sample coverage with block length chosen by pilot ACF decay.
\uses{(C5) $F$; KPI \textit{Realized FDR}/\textit{Provenance coverage} in \secref{subsec:ops-kpis}.}
\end{lemma}

\subsection{Leakage clamp FSM and EF guard}\label{subsec:clamp}
\emph{\layertag{Inf/Stoch/Comp}}

Compute leakage $\constref{chi}(t)$ (conditioning proxy). Enforce the local EF neighborhood \emph{before} any Fisher-quadratic use:

\begin{definition}[EF guard $\&$ clamp (Δ)]\label{def:ef-guard-op}
Run \texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds$(\varepsilon,\hat L_\psi,r)$}. On \textbf{pass}, emit a stable \texttt{ef\_guard\_id} and allow Fisher-quadratic proxies; on \textbf{fail}, \emph{fail-closed} to HSIC \& CVaR-only sensing. Apply the clamp FSM with deterministic thresholds:
\[
\text{Green: }\chi\le\constref{chiG},\quad
\text{Yellow: }\constref{chiG}<\chi\le\constref{chiR},\quad
\text{Red: }\chi>\constref{chiR},
\]
triggering shrink/relax of $(r,\cutq)$ exactly as Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}. The \texttt{ef\_guard\_id} is recorded in the window’s \emph{proof print} and propagated in KPI rows consumed by $\omega$-automata.
\uses{Thm.~\ref{thm:FIMbound}; Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}; (C10) thresholds.}
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Clamp determinism \& mirrors]\label{rem:clamp-determinism}
Clamp transitions are a pure function of $\chi(t)$ and pre-registered thresholds; mirrored signed sources in the Ladder must reproduce identical transitions on replay.
\uses{\secref{subsec:schemas}; \secref{subsec:ladder-prop}.}
\end{remark}

\subsection{Promotion discipline: MTC, IV-badge, and CIS rows}\label{subsec:mtc-iv}
\emph{\layertag{Inf/Stoch/Comp}}

\begin{definition}[Promotion gates]\label{def:mtc-iv}
A candidate index enters $E_t$ only if Rule~\ref{rule:promotion} passes: \emph{(i)} held-out transport improvement $\Delta \widehat R_{\mathrm{DRO}}\ge$ MDE at power $\ge0.8$; \emph{(ii)} SPRT on $M_t$ accepts low-coupling; \emph{(iii)} clamp=Green; \emph{(iv)} if using instruments, an IV-badge transcript (relevance $F>10$, preregistered exclusion across contexts, ablation sign) is attached. Record one CIS row per promotion with these fields.
\uses{\jsgiiDef{\lblMTC}; \jsgiiDef{\lblIVbadge}; Rule~\ref{rule:promotion}; Def.~\ref{def:tierpacks}.}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[CIS completeness]\label{prop:cis}
A CIS row is \emph{valid} iff it contains: BY-thresholded $p$; MTC transcript with achieved power; SPRT transcript \emph{with parameters} (below) and verdict; clamp state=Green; and IV-badge fields when applicable.
\uses{\jsgiiSec{\lblSchemas}; Rule~\ref{rule:promotion}; Def.~\ref{def:tierpacks}.}
\end{proposition}

\subsection{SPRT and the DRO envelope precondition}\label{subsec:sprt-dvdrho}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch/Inf/Comp}}

\begin{definition}[Envelope precondition]\label{def:env-pre}
Before running any SPRT on $M_t$, verify the DRO envelope identity $d\,\mathbb E[\varphi_\eta]/d\rho\approx \constref{etastar}$ within $5\%$; otherwise, quarantine the window and block promotions (Rule~\ref{rule:dvdrho}). Emit a \texttt{dvdrho\_id} for the proof print.
\uses{\jsgiiThm{\lblDROenv}; \jsgiiDef{\lblQuarantine}; Rule~\ref{rule:dvdrho}.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[SPRT parameterization (Δ)]\label{def:sprt-params}
For $M_t$, register $\texttt{sprt\_params}=\{H_0,B_{\mathrm{saf}},H_1,B_{\mathrm{risk}},\delta,\alpha,\beta\}$, where $H_0:\ M_t\le B_{\mathrm{saf}}$ and $H_1:\ M_t\ge B_{\mathrm{risk}}=B_{\mathrm{saf}}+\delta$. The same identifier appears in the proof print and CIS rows when a promotion is granted.
\uses{Rule~\ref{rule:promotion}; KPI in \secref{subsec:ops-kpis}.}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Well-posed SPRT]\label{lem:sprt-wellposed}
Given Defs.~\ref{def:env-pre}–\ref{def:sprt-params}, the sequential test on $M_t$ has controlled error $(\alpha,\beta)$ and monotone likelihood ratio in the low-coupling regime; transcripts must log stopping time and boundaries.
\uses{Rule~\ref{rule:promotion}; KPI in \secref{subsec:ops-kpis}.}
\end{lemma}

\section{Control Synthesis, Safety Invariance, and PR Certification (v2)}\label{sec:ctrl-v2}

\paragraph{Aim.}
Translate geometric and statistical contracts into \emph{auditable} control: a CLF/CBF-QP with frontier-aware relaxation and an explicit residual lower bound (LB), band-limited PR certificates with GKYP witnesses, consolidated switching-stability guards (with CQLF hash), and a measurable decision range $\hat R_t$ together with its numerically stable log-volume $\log \hat R_t$. Each window emits a control-side \emph{proof print} that includes \texttt{pr\_witness\_id}, \texttt{cbf\_audit\_id}, and \texttt{switch\_stability\_id} in the global EF$\rightarrow$DRO$\rightarrow$PR$\rightarrow$CBF$\rightarrow\omega$ order.

\subsection{Model, admissible set, and projection}\label{subsec:model-proj}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl/Rep}}

\begin{definition}[Piecewise control-affine strata]\label{def:pwa}
On each stratum $s$, the dynamics are locally control-affine:
\(
\dot x = f_s(x) + g_s(x)\,u,\quad u\in\mathcal U,
\)
with frontier tubes of radius $\constref{rtube}$ separating strata. Barriers $h_i(x)\ge 0$ encode rights-plane invariants.
\uses{(C1) $\constref{rtube}$; Rule~\ref{rule:frontier-freeze}.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Admissible control set and projection]\label{def:proj}
Let $\mathcal U=\{u: A_u u\le b_u\}$ be the actuator polytope and
\(
\mathcal U_{\mathrm{safe}}(x):=\{u\in\mathcal U:\ L_f h_i + L_g h_i\,u\ge -\gamma_i h_i\ \forall i\}
\)
the instantaneous safe set (off-tube). Define the projection
\(
\Pi_{\mathrm{safe}}(u):=\arg\min_{v\in \mathcal U_{\mathrm{safe}}(x)}\|v-u\|^2;
\)
inside frontier tubes apply the freeze rule (Rule~\ref{rule:frontier-freeze}) and the one-step relaxed audit (Lemma~\ref{lem:cbf-salt}).
\uses{Rule~\ref{rule:frontier-freeze}; Lem.~\ref{lem:cbf-salt}.}
\end{definition}

\subsection{CLF/CBF-QP with frontier-aware relaxation}\label{subsec:clf-cbf-qp}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl/Alg}}

\begin{lemma}[QP synthesis with explicit residual LB]\label{lem:qp}
Given a control Lyapunov function $V$ and barrier set $\{h_i\}$, solve at each step
\[
\begin{aligned}
\min_{u,\ s,\ \{\xi_i\}\ge 0}\quad
& \tfrac{1}{2}\|u-u_{\mathrm{ref}}\|^2 + \lambda_V s + \sum_i \lambda_i \xi_i \\
\text{s.t.}\quad
& L_f V + L_g V\,u \le -\alpha V + s,\\
& \begin{cases}
L_f h_i + L_g h_i\,u \ge -\gamma_i h_i - \xi_i,& \text{off-tube},\\[2pt]
h_i^+ \ge h_i^- - \sigma_i - \xi_i,\ \ \sigma_i=L_{h_i}\,\constsymsalt\,\|\delta x\|,& \text{inside tube},
\end{cases}\\
& A_u u \le b_u,\quad u\in\mathcal U.
\end{aligned}
\]
Define the \emph{residual lower bound}
\(
\mathrm{LB}_i:=h_i^+-h_i^-+\sigma_i
\)
for each barrier step inside tubes. If any $\mathrm{LB}_i<0$, \emph{reject the $\omega$-run} and set \texttt{cbf\_audit\_id} in the proof print.
\uses{Lemma~\ref{lem:cbf-salt}; (C11) $\constsymsalt$ and $\mathrm{CVaR}_{0.95}$; Rule~\ref{rule:frontier-freeze}.}
\end{lemma}

\begin{remark}[Rights-plane tail risk]\label{rem:cvar}
Log the distribution of barrier slacks and compute $\mathrm{CVaR}_{0.95}$; breach triggers Freeze\,+\,Dwell escalation (Rule~\ref{rule:rights-cvar}) and blocks promotions.
\uses{(C11) $\mathrm{CVaR}_{0.95}$; Rule~\ref{rule:rights-cvar}.}
\end{remark}

\subsection{Band-limited PR certification and auto-retune}\label{subsec:pr-cert}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl/Rep}}

Linearize on-window and certify $(\epsilon,[\constsymomegal,\constsymomegau])$-PR via GKYP; export $(\Omega,\epsilon,P,Q,\text{descriptor})$, a vertex hitmap, and a stable \texttt{pr\_witness\_id}. If realized margins approach $\constsymPRmargin$, automatically shrink $\Omega$ and re-certify before any control update.

\begin{proposition}[PR witness consumption]\label{prop:pr-consume}
Control updates are allowed only when a matching GKYP witness is present for the current uncertainty set; absent or crowded-margin witnesses force $\Omega$-shrink and re-certification \emph{before} actuation. The window proof print must include \texttt{pr\_witness\_id}.
\uses{Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}; (C12) $\constsymomegal,\constsymomegau,\constsymPRmargin$.}
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Switching stability for mixed-plane control (consolidated)}\label{subsec:switch}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl/Alg}}

\begin{lemma}[Degeneracy gate and stability witness]\label{lem:deg-stab}
Let $\Delta\sigma$ denote the spectral-gap change across a proposed basis update. If $\mathrm{CI}(\Delta\sigma)$ contains $0$, block the update. Otherwise require either (i) a CQLF certificate $P\succ 0$ that covers the active modes (emit \texttt{cqlf\_hash}), or (ii) a minimum switch-dwell $\tau_{\min}^{\mathrm{sw}}$. On failure, \emph{k}-escalate (Definition~\ref{def:hol-harm}). Record a stable \texttt{switch\_stability\_id} in the proof print.
\uses{Lemma~\ref{lem:switching}; (C3) dwell; (C9) \constref{Harm}.}
\end{lemma}

\subsection{Decision range computation and reporting}\label{subsec:range}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl/Rep}}

\begin{definition}[Estimated decision range and log-volume]\label{def:range}
Let $\mathcal U^\star_t$ be the active feasible set returned by the QP at time $t$ and $J_t:=\partial x_{t+1}/\partial u_t$ the local input map. Define
\[
\hat R_t\ :=\ \prod_{i\le r}\ \sigma_i\big(J_t|_{\mathcal U^\star_t}\big),\qquad
\log \hat R_t\ :=\ \sum_{i\le r}\ \log \sigma_i\big(J_t|_{\mathcal U^\star_t}\big).
\]
Report both $\hat R_t$ and $\log \hat R_t$ per window. Enforce \emph{conservative mode} (non-increasing $\hat R_t$) whenever clamp $\neq$ Green or PR margins crowd $\constsymPRmargin$ (Prop.~\ref{prop:cons-link}).
\uses{\secref{subsec:ops-kpis}; Prop.~\ref{prop:cons-link}.}
\end{definition}

\subsection{Safe lever selection (normative dictionary)}\label{subsec:levers}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl/Rep}}

\begin{rulex}{Lever policy}\label{rule:lever-policy}
Enumerate governance levers $\mathcal L$ (e.g., TRO/PI, SLA, anti-commandeering, audit scope). Run safe-UCB over $\mathcal L$ with feasibility set
\(
\{\text{QP feasible} \wedge \text{CVaR tails OK} \wedge \text{PR witness valid}\}
\)
and reward equal to \emph{curvature-per-collateral} (ISS improvement per unit rights/fiscal/holonomy cost). Log chosen lever and anchors.
\uses{Rule~\ref{rule:levers}; (C11) CVaR; (C12) PR certificate.}
\end{rulex}

\section{Deployment and Self-Scaling (v2)}\label{sec:deploy-v2}

\paragraph{Objective.}
Ship a \emph{deterministic} bring-up that boots sensing $\to$ guards $\to$ control $\to$ evidence with guardrails on, then run closed-loop \emph{self-scaling} that (i) tightens EF/FDR when conditioning degrades, (ii) auto-retunes PR bands using GKYP hitmaps, and (iii) manages dwell/tube hygiene from grazing diagnostics—while respecting compute/SLO budgets and leaving a replayable trace. Every window emits a \emph{proof print} (IDs only) showing EF$\rightarrow$DRO$\rightarrow$PR$\rightarrow$CBF$\rightarrow\omega$ consumption in order.

\subsection{Environment pinning and reproducible builds}\label{subsec:pinning}
\emph{\layertag{Comp/Rep}}

\begin{definition}[Pinned environment]\label{def:pinned-env}
All deployments must pin: container image (SHA), solver versions/tolerances, PRNG seeds, CPU/GPU flags. The pinned tuple
\[
\langle\texttt{replay\_pack\_sha},\ \texttt{seeds},\ \texttt{solver\_transcript\_id}\rangle
\]
is recorded in Tier-1 (Tab.~\ref{tab:tier1-schema}). KPI rows additionally carry \texttt{rollout\_mode} $\in\{\text{Shadow},\text{Canary},\text{BlueGreen}\}$ and a per-window \texttt{proof\_print\_id}. 
\uses{\jsgiiSec{\lblSchemas}; \secref{subsec:det-replay}.}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Deterministic cold boot]\label{prop:cold-boot}
A cold boot that ingests a reference telemetry slice and runs one window must reproduce: FIM/whitening hashes, $M_t$, clamp verdict, and (if enabled) a PR witness within numeric tolerance. Let $\delta_{\mathrm{fp}}^{\mathrm{abs}}=10^{-12}$ and $\delta_{\mathrm{fp}}^{\mathrm{rel}}=10^{-9}$; value-wise checks pass if
\(
|x^{\mathrm{replay}}\!-\!x^{\mathrm{ref}}|\le \delta_{\mathrm{fp}}^{\mathrm{abs}}+\delta_{\mathrm{fp}}^{\mathrm{rel}}|x^{\mathrm{ref}}|
\).
Deviations require an annotated solver/runtime-change note.
\uses{Rem.~\ref{rem:white-hash}; Prop.~\ref{prop:pr-consume}.}
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Bring-up script (five phases)}\label{subsec:bringup}
\emph{\layertag{Comp/Inf/Ctrl}}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Telemetry \& hashing.} Start windowing of length $\constref{tau}$; create \texttt{window\_id} and compute \texttt{FIM\_hash}/\texttt{whitening\_hash}.
\uses{Def.~\ref{def:tierpacks}; Rem.~\ref{rem:white-hash}.}
\item \textbf{EF guard on.} Estimate $\hat L_\psi$; set $r=\varepsilon/\hat L_\psi$ and run \texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds}; initialize clamp FSM to Green; stamp \texttt{ef\_guard\_id}.
\uses{Thm.~\ref{thm:FIMbound}; Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}.}
\item \textbf{Control core.} Enable CLF/CBF-QP with frontier-aware audit; start CVaR monitoring; freeze basis in tubes; stamp \texttt{cbf\_audit\_id}.
\uses{Lemma~\ref{lem:cbf-salt}; Rule~\ref{rule:frontier-freeze}; Rule~\ref{rule:rights-cvar}.}
\item \textbf{PR witness.} Linearize on-window; solve GKYP; store $(\Omega,\epsilon,P,Q,\text{descriptor})$ and vertex hitmap; stamp \texttt{pr\_witness\_id}.
\uses{Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}.}
\item \textbf{DRO/SPRT gate (quarantinable).} Fit soft-transport; verify envelope $d\mathbb E[\varphi_\eta]/d\rho\approx \constref{etastar}$; if fail, set \texttt{quarantine\_bit}=1 and skip promotion logic; stamp \texttt{dvdrho\_id}.
\uses{Prop.~\ref{prop:kkt}; Rule~\ref{rule:dvdrho}.}
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Proof print (per-window).}
Emit a compact record \texttt{proof\_print\_id} $:= H(\texttt{ef\_guard\_id}\!\Vert\!\texttt{dvdrho\_id}\!\Vert\!\texttt{pr\_witness\_id}\!\Vert\!\texttt{cbf\_audit\_id}\!\Vert\!\texttt{omega\_monitor\_id})$ that the automata also ingest; any guard-order violation fails closed for that window.
\uses{\secref{subsec:automata}; \secref{subsec:det-replay}.}

\subsection{Self-scaling loops}\label{subsec:self-scaling}
\emph{\layertag{Inf/Ctrl/Rep}}

\paragraph{(A) EF/FDR clamp loop.}
Every window, compute leakage $\constref{chi}(t)$ and realized FDR from BY null audits. Let hysteresis constants be pre-registered as
\(
(\chi_G,\chi_Y,\chi_R)=(\constref{chiG},\constref{chiY},\constref{chiR})
\)
and FDR relax/tighten policy $(0.9\cutq,\ 2\text{ windows})$.

\begin{rulex}{Clamp-driven EF radius and FDR adaptation}\label{rule:clamp-adapt}
If clamp enters Yellow: shrink $(r,\cutq)$ deterministically by factors (e.g., $r\!\leftarrow\!0.8r$, $\cutq\!\leftarrow\!0.8\cutq$), freeze promotions. If Red: fail-closed to HSIC/CVaR-only. When Green for two consecutive windows and realized FDR $\le 0.9\cutq$, allow $(r,\cutq)$ to relax by at most $10\%$ per window. All transitions appear in Tier-1 and Ladder parity.
\uses{Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}; \secref{subsec:ops-kpis}.}
\end{rulex}

\paragraph{(B) PR band auto-retune.}
Consume the GKYP vertex hitmap to adapt the uncertainty set and band endpoints.

\begin{rulex}{Hitmap-driven PR retune}\label{rule:pr-retune}
If realized PR margin approaches $\constsymPRmargin$, shrink $\Omega$ toward hit vertices; re-certify and stamp a fresh \texttt{pr\_witness\_id}. If margin $>\constsymPRmargin$ for $k$ windows and clamp stays Green, widen $\Omega$ one notch (pre-registered step).
\uses{Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}; Prop.~\ref{prop:pr-consume}; (C12) $\constsymPRmargin$.}
\end{rulex}

\paragraph{(C) Dwell/tube hygiene loop.}
Use grazing diagnostics to adjust dwell and tube radius.

\begin{rulex}{Grazing-driven dwell/tube retune}\label{rule:dwell-retune}
If $\mathrm{CI}(\constref{nuhat})$ touches $0$ or $\constref{pgraze}>\constref{pthr}$, set $\constref{taumin}\!\leftarrow\!(1+\constref{gammadwell})\constref{taumin}$ and $\constref{rtube}\!\leftarrow\!\constref{rtube}/(1+\constref{gammadwell})$; log tube-id and timestamps. Revert only after two windows with positive normal-speed CI and no chatter.
\uses{Rule~\ref{rule:grazing}; \secref{subsec:freeze-hygiene}.}
\end{rulex}

\subsection{Resource sizing and real-time budgets}\label{subsec:res-sizing}
\emph{\layertag{Comp/Rep}}

\begin{definition}[Per-window compute budget]\label{def:budget}
Let $T_{\mathrm{win}}$ be the wall-clock deadline per window. Enforce
\[
T_{\mathrm{fim}}+T_{\mathrm{cum}}+T_{\mathrm{guard}}+T_{\mathrm{qp}}+T_{\mathrm{gkyp}}+T_{\mathrm{dro}}\ \le\ T_{\mathrm{win}},
\]
with timeouts \(T_{\mathrm{qp}}\le \alpha_{\mathrm{qp}}T_{\mathrm{win}},\ \ T_{\mathrm{gkyp}}\le \alpha_{\mathrm{gkyp}}T_{\mathrm{win}},\ \ T_{\mathrm{dro}}\le \alpha_{\mathrm{dro}}T_{\mathrm{win}}\) (e.g., $0.25,0.25,0.25$). The budget guard prints \texttt{budget\_guard\_verdict} $\in\{\text{OK},\text{TIMEOUT}\}$ in Tier-1.
\uses{\jsgiiSec{\lblSchemas} (solver transcripts).}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Backlog and SLO]\label{prop:slo}
Maintain queue length $\le 1$ window and on-time ratio $\ge 99\%$. Any timeout triggers conservative mode: disable promotions, keep control with last verified PR witness, and skip noncritical solvers until debt is cleared.
\uses{Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}; Prop.~\ref{prop:pr-consume}.}
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[Sizing hints]\label{rem:sizing}
Choose block length for MBB/CBB from pilot ACF; pick HAC bandwidth to keep $T_{\mathrm{guard}}$ sublinear in features. Prefer Cholesky for well-conditioned FIMs; fall back to SVD if condition number exceeds a pre-registered threshold.
\uses{\secref{subsec:coverage}.}
\end{remark}

\section{Governance Anchors and Lever Dictionary (v2)}\label{sec:gov-v2}

\paragraph{Aim.}
Expose policy as \emph{provable} controller contracts. Each lever has (i) a \emph{policy clause}, (ii) a \emph{control translation} (constraints, margins, guards), (iii) \emph{evidence hooks} (Tier-1/2 fields), and (iv) \emph{unwind criteria}. Selection uses the safe-UCB rule in \secref{subsec:levers}; feasibility is enforced by CLF/CBF-QP (Lemma~\ref{lem:cbf-salt}) and PR/GKYP witnesses (Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}). \subsection{Lever ABI (application binary interface)}\label{subsec:lever-abi}
\emph{\layertag{Rep/Ctrl}}

\begin{definition}[Lever ABI]\label{def:lever-abi}
A lever $L$ is a tuple
\(
L=\langle\texttt{name},\ \texttt{scope},\ \texttt{guardset},\ \texttt{qp\_mods},\ \texttt{pr\_mods},\ \texttt{kpi\_targets},\ \texttt{evidence},\ \texttt{unwind}\rangle
\)
compiled to:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{QP modifications} \texttt{qp\_mods}: edits to $\mathcal U$, barrier gains $(\gamma_i)$, residual budgets, or additional linearized constraints.
\item \textbf{PR/GKYP modifications} \texttt{pr\_mods}: band tightening $[\constsymomegal,\constsymomegau]$, polytope shrink $\Omega\mapsto\Omega'$, margin floor $\epsilon\ge \constsymPRmargin$.
\item \textbf{Guardset} \texttt{guardset}: preconditions that must remain true (EF guard Green, quarantine off, CVaR nonnegative).
\item \textbf{KPI targets} \texttt{kpi\_targets}: explicit bounds to be consumed by ω-automata (Sec.~\ref{subsec:automata}).
\item \textbf{Evidence} \texttt{evidence}: Tier-1/2 fields and solver transcripts to log (hash IDs, verdicts).
\item \textbf{Unwind}: deterministic criteria to revert the lever.
\end{enumerate}
\uses{Lemma~\ref{lem:cbf-salt}; Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}; Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}; Rule~\ref{rule:dvdrho}; \secref{subsec:automata}.}
\end{definition}

\section{Related Work and Positioning (v2)}\label{sec:related-v2}

\paragraph{Aim.}

\subsection{Comparison axes}\label{subsec:rel-axes}
\emph{\layertag{Rep}}
We contrast methods along six axes that matter operationally:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Certificate type.} Static (offline) vs.\ dynamic (windowed) and \emph{consumed} by run-time logic.
\item \textbf{Identifiability discipline.} Discovery gates synchronized with EF neighborhoods (Thm.~\ref{thm:FIMbound}) and DRO duality (Prop.~\ref{prop:kkt}) vs.\ correlation-only triggers.
\item \textbf{Non-smooth geometry.} Frontiers, saltation, and No-Zeno resource accounting (Lemmas~\ref{lem:nozeno-impl}, \ref{lem:cbf-salt}).
\item \textbf{Control robustness.} Band-limited PR via GKYP under explicit uncertainty sets and switching stability (Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}; Lemma~\ref{lem:switching}).

\item \textbf{Provenance.} Deterministic replays: container SHA, seeds, solver tolerances, and Tier-1…4 chain (Def.~\ref{def:tierpacks}; \secref{subsec:schemas}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Position relative to canonical tools}\label{subsec:rel-canon}
\emph{\layertag{Rep/Inf/Ctrl}}

\paragraph{(i) Passivity/ISS and GKYP/PR.}
Classical passivity $\Rightarrow$ ISS is well known. JSGIII adopts it as the global scaffold (Prop.~\ref{prop:iss-spend}) and strengthens \emph{operationality} by: (a) certifying PR \emph{on a band} with explicit uncertainty sets (Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}); (b) auto-shrinking $\Omega$ when margins crowd \constsymPRmargin\ (Prop.~\ref{prop:pr-consume}); and (c) printing the frontier spend term alongside the ISS budget each window (Prop.~\ref{prop:iss-spend}).

\paragraph{(ii) CBF/CLF-QP on hybrid/non-smooth systems.}
Barrier methods typically assume smooth flows. JSGIII elevates tube-level non-smoothness to \emph{first-class} status via a numeric saltation bound and a one-step residual audit (Lemma~\ref{lem:cbf-salt}) integrated with Frontier Freeze and Dwell Escalation (Rules~\ref{rule:frontier-freeze}, \ref{rule:grazing}). Rights-plane safety is then \emph{audited} by residual LB$\ge0$ and CVaR tails (Rule~\ref{rule:rights-cvar}).

\paragraph{(iii) EF neighborhoods and KL–Fisher quadratic.}
Local quadratic surrogates are routine in information geometry; JSGIII operationalizes them with a guard that is checked/logged every window: $r=\varepsilon/L_\psi$ and \texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds} (Thm.~\ref{thm:FIMbound}, Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}). A three-state clamp (Green/Yellow/Red) then \emph{drives} $(r,\cutq)$ and promotion policy.

\paragraph{(iv) DRO duality and envelope/sensitivity.}
Danskin/envelope identities are standard dual tools; JSGIII \emph{consumes} them as a run-time precondition before SPRT/MTC logic (Prop.~\ref{prop:kkt}, Rule~\ref{rule:dvdrho}). Failure deterministically sets \texttt{quarantine\_bit} and freezes promotions (Def.~\ref{def:window-quarantine-impl}).

\paragraph{(v) Sequential testing and FDR control.}
SPRTs and BY-FDR are classical. JSGIII couples them to geometry and model health: the SPRT runs only if the DRO envelope passes and clamp=Green (Rule~\ref{rule:promotion}); realized-FDR feeds the clamp loop that shrinks $(r,\cutq)$ (Rule~\ref{rule:clamp-adapt}).

\paragraph{(vi) Instruments/IV diagnostics.}
Econometric IV practice motivates the IV-badge in our promotion gates: relevance ($F>10$), preregistered exclusion across contexts, and micro-ablation sign; badges are logged per CIS row (Rule~\ref{rule:promotion}, Prop.~\ref{prop:cis}).

\paragraph{(vii) Subspace tracking, DK/Wedin drift, and holonomy.}
Davis–Kahan/Wedin perturbation bounds are classical; JSGIII records \emph{angle/gap} tuples per crossing and budgets \emph{Harmonic Rotational Mass}, triggering $k$-escalation when degeneracy rises (Def.~\ref{def:hol-harm}, Lemma~\ref{lem:usc-planes}).

\paragraph{(viii) Formal methods (LTL/$\omega$-automata).}
Runtime assurance via temporal logic is well-studied. Our twist is to make automata consume KPI \emph{row-hashes} from Tier-2, producing bit-match accept/reject transcripts (\secref{subsec:automata}).

\paragraph{(ix) Provenance, reproducibility, and evidence packs.}
Determinism via container pins and seeds is common; JSGIII requires solver transcripts and enforces \emph{schema-complete} Tier-1 packs with CI failure on any missing field (Tab.~\ref{tab:tier1-schema}, \secref{subsec:schemas}).

\section{Conclusion, Limits, and Audit Surface (v2)}\label{sec:conclusion-v2}

\paragraph{Summary.}
JSGIII instantiates a \emph{proof-carrying governor}: sensing $\to$ guards $\to$ control $\to$ evidence, each step falsifiable by replay. Two closures operationalize non-smooth geometry (priced frontier defects with No-Zeno; DK/Wedin-backed plane drift) while EF/KL and DRO-envelope checks gate statistics; GKYP witnesses gate control; ω-automata consume KPI row-hashes. Measured effects are (i) reduction in coupling mass $M_t$, (ii) expansion of decision range $\hat R_t$ subject to I\textsubscript{5}, and (iii) rights-plane safety with deterministic fail-closed behavior. Throughout this section, $\varepsilon$ denotes the \emph{EF tolerance} used in $r=\varepsilon/L_\psi$ (Thm.~\ref{thm:FIMbound}); the PR margin floor is denoted by \constsymPRmargin\ (Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}).

\subsection{What is guaranteed, and where it is enforced}\label{subsec:claims}
\emph{\layertag{Rep}}

We organize the governance guarantees I\(_1\)–I\(_5\) as verifiable invariants with concrete enforcers and artifacts:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]

No-Zeno dwell (Lemma~\ref{lem:nozeno-impl}) bounds crossing rate and prevents chatter. Evidence: $\mathrm{LB}_i$ series, CVaR tails, freeze/dwell timestamps in Tier-1.
\uses{Lem.~\ref{lem:nozeno-impl}, \ref{lem:cbf-salt}; Rule~\ref{rule:frontier-freeze}; Rule~\ref{rule:grazing}.}

\item \textbf{I\(_2\) Statistical locality (EF).}
All Fisher-quadratic uses are guarded by \texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds} inside $r=\varepsilon/L_\psi$ with clamp-driven adaptation (Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}); failures go \emph{fail-closed} to HSIC/CVaR-only sensing. Evidence: guard verdict $(\varepsilon,\hat L_\psi,r)$ and clamp transcript in Tier-1/Tier-2.

\item \textbf{I\(_3\) Identifiability-first discovery.}
Promotion gates (MTC $>$ correlation) fire only if the DRO envelope passes and clamp=Green (Rule~\ref{rule:promotion}; Prop.~\ref{prop:kkt}); otherwise windows are quarantined. Evidence: CIS rows with BY-$p$, power, SPRT transcript $(\alpha,\beta,H_0,H_1)$, IV-badge (if used), and \texttt{quarantine\_bit}=0.
\uses{Rule~\ref{rule:promotion}; Prop.~\ref{prop:kkt}; Def.~\ref{def:mtc-iv}; Prop.~\ref{prop:cis}.}

\item \textbf{I\(_4\) Actuation-basis stability.}
Holonomy/Harmonic Rotational Mass budget plane drift; rising HarmMass blocks basis updates or forces $k$-escalation/switch-dwell (Lemma~\ref{lem:usc-planes}, Prop.~\ref{prop:hol-harm}, Lemma~\ref{lem:deg-stab}). Evidence: SVD tuples/gap ledger and escalation transcript.
\uses{Lem.~\ref{lem:usc-planes}; Prop.~\ref{prop:hol-harm}; Lem.~\ref{lem:deg-stab}.}

\item \textbf{I\(_5\) Rights-plane and PR stability.}
Band-limited PR witnesses (GKYP) are required \emph{before} any control update; when margins crowd \constsymPRmargin, $\Omega$ auto-shrinks and re-certifies (Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}, \ref{prop:pr-consume}). Rights-plane tail risk is audited by $\mathrm{CVaR}_{0.95}$. Evidence: $(\Omega,\epsilon,P,Q)$ and hitmap ids, CVaR slacks.
\uses{Prop.~\ref{prop:gkyp-witness}, \ref{prop:pr-consume}; Rem.~\ref{rem:cvar}.}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Threats to validity and scope limits}\label{subsec:limits}
\emph{\layertag{Rep}}

\paragraph{Locality of EF neighborhoods.}
KL–Fisher quadratic equivalence is local; outside $r=\varepsilon/L_\psi$ proxies can mislead. Mitigation: clamp shrink/relax, fail-closed to HSIC/CVaR, and Tier-1 evidence that a \emph{passing} guard precedes any Fisher-quadratic use.
\uses{Thm.~\ref{thm:FIMbound}; Rule~\ref{rule:ef-clamp}; Tab.~\ref{tab:tier1-schema}.}

\paragraph{Quasi-stationarity and windowing.}
Variance panels (HAC/MBB/CBB) assume mixing; fast shifts degrade coverage. Mitigation: realized-FDR audits tighten $q$, envelope failures quarantine windows, and ω-monitors continue enforcing safety specs.
\uses{\secref{subsec:coverage}; Rule~\ref{rule:clamp-adapt}; Def.~\ref{def:env-pre}; \secref{subsec:automata}.}

\paragraph{Hybrid geometry assumptions.}
Frontier calculus presumes tubes, transversal crossings, and Filippov well-posedness. Unmodeled non-Filippov discontinuities are out-of-scope; saltation replay will fail and the run is rejected.
\uses{Lem.~\ref{lem:nozeno-impl}, \ref{lem:cbf-salt}; \secref{subsec:freeze-hygiene}.}

\paragraph{Spectral degeneracy.}
When gaps collapse, DK/Wedin bounds loosen. We block updates and enforce dwell/CQLF; any actuation without a stability witness is disallowed.
\uses{Lem.~\ref{lem:deg-stab}.}

\paragraph{Instrument validity (IV).}
IV-badges rely on a finite Context Registry; latent, unregistered context drift can erode exclusion. We require invariance tests across preregistered contexts and deny badges absent power.
\uses{Def.~\ref{def:mtc-iv}; Prop.~\ref{prop:cis}.}

\paragraph{Compute/SLO limits.}
On solver timeouts we flip to conservative mode (no promotions; last verified PR witness; skip noncritical solvers) until debt clears.
\uses{Def.~\ref{def:budget}; Prop.~\ref{prop:slo}.}

\paragraph{Adversarial telemetry.}
Mirrored sources and parity checks catch many—but not all—tampering modes; synchronized forgeries are out-of-scope. Non-matching parity induces quarantine and freeze; automata reject quarantined consumption.
\uses{\secref{subsec:schemas}, \ref{subsec:ladder-prop}.}

\subsection{Reproducibility and legal legibility}\label{subsec:repro}
\emph{\layertag{Rep/Comp}}

A deployment is \emph{audit-ready} iff: (i) every consumed window has a Tier-1 pack that passes Tab.~\ref{tab:tier1-schema}; (ii) GKYP/CBF/DRO solver transcripts re-verify; (iii) automata accept/reject on KPI row-hashes without raw data; (iv) cold boot matches FIM/whitening hashes, $M_t$, clamp state, and (if present) PR witness to tolerance; and (v) each window prints a compact \emph{proof-print id} encoding the EF$\rightarrow$DRO$\rightarrow$PR$\rightarrow$CBF guard order consumed (Prop.~\ref{prop:guard-top}).
\uses{\secref{subsec:schemas}, \secref{subsec:det-replay}, \secref{subsec:automata}; Prop.~\ref{prop:cold-boot}, \ref{prop:guard-top}.}
