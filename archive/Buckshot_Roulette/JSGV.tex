% =============================== Abstract ===============================
\begin{abstract}
We replace brittle heuristic stacking with a unified calculus grounded in three pillars: (i) a \emph{bimonoidal product poset} $Q$ for safety grades, with vertical aggregation $(Q,\vmeet)$ as coordinatewise max and horizontal aggregation $(Q,\hplus)$ as a mixed operator (sum on \cvar, \ledger; max elsewhere), tied together by a \emph{lax interchange law} $(x\vmeet x')\hplus(y\vmeet y') \,\geqQ\, (x\hplus y)\vmeet(x'\hplus y')$; (ii) a typed effect system $\Sig$ for stateful obligations equipped with a \emph{total discharge relation} \code{DischargedBy(obligation, stage, witness)} and a \emph{well-founded multiset measure} proving progress; and (iii) a composition model \composeev{} as a \emph{lax double functor} from designs to evidence, whose \emph{lax exchange law} holds under explicit side-conditions (stage monotonicity, lax-bilinearity of $\hplus$, non-expansive retunes, ordered clamps).

We make the framework review-tight with: (a) a \emph{risk composition policy} for \cvar{} (default summation; optional convolution/copula paths gated by certified dependence witnesses in $\Sig$); (b) a strict \emph{validation order} (schema check $\rightarrow$ form $Q$ $\rightarrow$ aggregate), clarifying when $\unk$ propagates vs.\ when runs hard-fail; (c) a canonical, hash-chained \code{proof\_print} with a complete \emph{determinism envelope} (PRNG seeds, library SHAs, IEEE-754 mode and NaN canonicalization, endianness, canonical ordering, WCET traces) and explicit versioning fields. A CI-ready invariant suite (\IMeet, \IMono, \ISigma, \IExchange, \IClamp, \IReplay) plus golden fixtures and property-based tests lift the laws into continuously checked practice. Governance becomes \emph{enforceable by construction}: overrides and retunes are typed, scoped effects that alter $\Sig$ and the final hash; audits become deterministic replays of \composeev{}’s evidence.
\end{abstract}

\section{The Inevitable Failure of Heuristic Stacking}
\label{sec:failure-of-stacking}

\subsection{What We Mean by Heuristic Stacking}
A \emph{heuristic} is a locally sensible guard, threshold, or checklist applied to a subsystem or stage. \emph{Stacking} arises when safety posture is improved over time by simply adding more such guards, each with its own score, threshold, and ad-hoc aggregation rule. Stacking feels expedient, but it lacks a \emph{shared semantics}: scores are incomparable, order effects are uncontrolled, and audit artifacts are non-canonical. In short, the global outcome is no longer a predictable function of the parts.

\subsection{Four Pathologies of an Ad-Hoc Architecture}
\paragraph{(P1) Non-compositionality.}
Outcomes depend on evaluation order (e.g., a stability retune can reduce barrier slack); integrators cannot reason modularly. Local improvements can worsen the integrated system because no algebra constrains \emph{vertical} (sequential) and \emph{horizontal} (wiring) interactions.

\paragraph{(P2) Semantic drift.}
New, bespoke scores appear without normalization; bigger is worse is not enforced across stages; sums, maxima, and percentiles intermix without law. Aggregation ceases to be monotone, making comparison and policy tuning brittle.

\paragraph{(P3) Audit opacity.}
Artifacts are heterogeneous and non-deterministic; replay requires manual reconstruction. Without canonical witnesses and a determinism envelope, hashes cannot certify provenance, and small vendor/library changes become invisible.

\paragraph{(P4) Governance theater.}
Policies live as prose or comments, not as machine-enforced invariants. Silent overrides and undocumented retunes are possible; preconditions and expiries are not first-class and cannot be checked compositionally.

\subsection{Derived Requirements (What the Calculus Must Provide)}
To replace stacking, a unifying calculus must satisfy the following \emph{engineering} requirements:

\begin{enumerate}[label=R\arabic*., leftmargin=2.2em]
  \item \textbf{Universal grade space.} A normalized, dimensionless product poset $\Q$ with bigger is worse order, an explicit \emph{unknown} $\unk$, and two monoids: vertical $(\Q,\vmeet)$ for sequential stages and horizontal $(\Q,\hplus)$ for wiring.
  \item \textbf{Lawful interaction.} An \emph{interchange} (lax bilinearity) law relating $\vmeet$ and $\hplus$ to bound order effects in integration.
  \item \textbf{Monotone normalization.} Each guard supplies a monotone map into designated coordinates of $\Q$; registry of coordinates documents vertical/horizontal ops (e.g., \ledger/\cvar{} sum; others max).
  \item \textbf{Validation order.} Strict sequence: \emph{schema check} $\rightarrow$ \emph{form $\Q$} $\rightarrow$ \emph{aggregate}. Schema-required fields fail hard \emph{before} $\Q$; optional metrics may produce $\unk$ which then absorbs under aggregation.
  \item \textbf{Typed obligations.} A logic $\Sig$ that captures preconditions, scopes, expiries, and discharge rules (\code{DischargedBy}); a well-founded progress measure to guarantee termination in passing windows.
  \item \textbf{Deterministic evidence.} A canonical, hash-chained \code{proof\_print} with fixed stage order (\EF{}$\!\to$\DRO{}$\!\to$\PR{}$\!\to$\CBF{}$\!\to$\om), versioned schema, canonical byte encoding, and a complete determinism envelope.
  \item \textbf{Enforceable governance.} Overrides and retunes are typed, scoped effects that alter $\Sig$ and the final hash; clamps are monotone; the $\om$ meta-monitor enforces window-level preconditions (e.g., $K$ policy).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The Remedy: A Small Set of Enforceable Laws}
We adopt a three-pillar calculus that meets R1–R7:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Algebra of Acceptance ($\Q$).} A \emph{bimonoidal product poset} with $(\Q,\vmeet)$ as coordinatewise max (idempotent, commutative) and $(\Q,\hplus)$ as a mixed operator (sum on \cvar{} and \ledger; max elsewhere). The \emph{lax interchange law}
  \[
    (x \vmeet x') \hplus (y \vmeet y') \;\geqQ\; (x \hplus y) \vmeet (x' \hplus y')
  \]
  bounds order effects and underwrites integration safety.
  \item \textbf{Logic of Obligations ($\Sig$).} Obligations are typed, scoped effects with a total \code{DischargedBy} relation and a well-founded multiset measure ensuring \emph{progress}. Governance is encoded as theorems (e.g., \code{RetuneDisabledInRed}, \code{RequiresHitmap}, \code{OmegaPrecond}).
  \item \textbf{Geometry of Composition (\composeev).} A \emph{lax double functor} mapping designs to evidence, preserving vertical/horizontal composition up to $\geqQ$; its \emph{lax exchange law} holds under explicit side conditions (stage monotonicity, lax-bilinearity of $\hplus$, non-expansive retunes, ordered clamps).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Why This Fixes P1–P4}
Non-compositionality (P1) is addressed by lawful vertical/horizontal monoids and the interchange bound; semantic drift (P2) by a normalized coordinate registry and monotone homomorphisms into $\Q$; audit opacity (P3) by a canonical \code{proof\_print} and determinism envelope; governance theater (P4) by $\Sig$’s typed, auditable effects and CI-checked invariants (\IMeet, \IMono, \ISigma, \IExchange, \IClamp, \IReplay).

\section{The Algebra of Acceptance ($\Q$): Formal Semantics for Safety Grades}
\label{sec:algebra-Q}

\subsection{From Scores to a Bimonoidal Product Poset}
Safety aggregation must respect two orthogonal compositions: \emph{vertical} (sequential stages within a window) and \emph{horizontal} (wiring disjoint subsystems). We model grades in a normalized, dimensionless product poset $\Q$ equipped with two commutative monoids:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $(\Q,\vmeet)$ for vertical aggregation (idempotent, commutative, associative).
  \item $(\Q,\hplus)$ for horizontal aggregation (commutative, associative), with mixed coordinate rules.
\end{itemize}
The interaction is governed by a \emph{lax interchange} inequality that bounds order effects and underwrites \cref{sec:compose-ev}.

\subsection{Grade Space, Order, and the Unknown}
Let $I$ index coordinates (e.g., $\chi$, \powerInv, \sensErr, \dualGap, \prMarginInv, \minLBDef, \code{salt}, \ledger, \cvar, $\knorm$). Define
\[
  \Q \coloneqq \prod_{i\in I} Q_i, \qquad Q_i \coloneqq \RR_{\ge 0}\cup\{\unk\}.
\]
Interpretation is \emph{bigger is worse}. We order grades componentwise by
\[
  x \geqQ y \iff \forall i\in I:\; x_i \ge y_i \quad\text{with}\quad \unk \text{ as the top element (absorbing)}.
\]
Thus $(\Q,\geqQ)$ is a product poset whose top is the all-$\unk$ vector. Safety is fail-closed: any composition with $\unk$ yields $\unk$.

\subsection{Vertical Monoid $(\Q,\vmeet)$: Sequential Aggregation}
\begin{definition}[Vertical operator]
For $x,y\in\Q$, define $(x\vmeet y)_i \coloneqq \max(x_i,y_i)$ (with $\max(\unk,a)=\unk$).
\end{definition}
\begin{lemma}[Laws of $(\Q,\vmeet)$]\label{lem:meet-laws}
$(\Q,\vmeet)$ is a commutative, idempotent monoid with identity $0$ (the all-zero vector). Moreover, $\unk$ is absorbing: $x\vmeet\unk=\unk$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{invariant}[\IMeet]
Vertical aggregation is associative, commutative, idempotent, and absorbs $\unk$.
\end{invariant}

\subsection{Horizontal Monoid $(\Q,\hplus)$: Wiring Disjoint Subsystems}
Some risks accumulate (\ledger, \cvar); most take worst-case.
\begin{definition}[Horizontal operator]
For $x,y\in\Q$ and coordinate $i$,
\[
  (x\hplus y)_i \coloneqq
  \begin{cases}
    x_i + y_i, & i\in\{\ledger,\cvar\},\\[0.2em]
    \max(x_i,y_i), & \text{otherwise},
  \end{cases}
  \qquad \text{with } \unk \text{ absorbing.}
\]
\end{definition}
\begin{lemma}[Laws of $(\Q,\hplus)$]\label{lem:hplus-laws}
$(\Q,\hplus)$ is a commutative monoid with identity $0$, and $\unk$ is absorbing: $x\hplus\unk=\unk$.
\end{lemma}

\subsection{Interchange (Lax Bilinearity) and Monotonicity}
\begin{proposition}[Lax interchange law]\label{prop:lax-interchange}
For all $x,x',y,y'\in\Q$,
\[
  (x\vmeet x') \hplus (y\vmeet y') \;\geqQ\; (x\hplus y)\vmeet(x'\hplus y').
\]
\emph{Proof sketch.} Coordinatewise. For max–max coordinates, both sides equal $\max(x_i,x'_i,y_i,y'_i)$. For sum–max coordinates ($i\in\{\ledger,\cvar\}$), the claim reduces to
$\max(x_i,x'_i) + \max(y_i,y'_i) \ge \max(x_i+y_i,\,x'_i+y'_i)$, which holds by elementary case analysis.
\qed
\end{proposition}

\begin{invariant}[\IMono]
All stage and adapter maps into $\Q$ are monotone w.r.t.\ $\geqQ$; tightening any guard cannot improve the aggregated grade.
\end{invariant}

\subsection{Normalization: Monotone Homomorphisms into \texorpdfstring{$\Q$}{Q}}
Each guard contributes \emph{monotone} coordinates via simple, law-abiding maps (examples):
\[
\powerInv=\max(0,1-\mathrm{power}),\quad
\prMarginInv=\frac{1}{\max(\mathrm{PRmargin},\varepsilon)},\quad
\minLBDef=\max(0,-\min_i LB_i).
\]
Adapters \emph{must not} leak raw scores; instead they publish monotone images into named coordinates, documented in a registry.

\subsection{Risk Composition Policy for \texorpdfstring{\cvar}{cvar}}
The default horizontal rule $\cvar{:}$ \emph{sum} is a conservative, coherent upper bound (comonotone worst case). Less pessimistic aggregation is permitted only under explicit, auditable dependence certificates carried in $\Sig$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{IndependenceCert} $\Rightarrow$ compute aggregate loss via convolution (then evaluate CVaR).
  \item \textbf{MarginalsOnlyCert} $\Rightarrow$ remain at the Fréchet upper bound (sum).
  \item \textbf{CopulaCert($\theta$)} $\Rightarrow$ construct aggregate via the certified copula; evaluate CVaR.
\end{itemize}
Attempting to use a non-default rule emits \code{RequiresDepCert(CVaR)}; \Sig{} discharge enforces the choice and records it in evidence.

\subsection{Validation Order: Schema \texorpdfstring{$\to$}{->} Form \texorpdfstring{$\Q$}{Q} \texorpdfstring{$\to$}{->} Aggregate}
To separate contract violations from metric uncertainty:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(V\arabic*)]
  \item \textbf{Schema check.} REQUIRED fields validated. Failure \emph{short-circuits} with a hard runtime error; no $\Q$ is formed.
  \item \textbf{Form $\Q$.} Optional missing metrics map to $\unk$ at their coordinates (fail-closed).
  \item \textbf{Aggregate.} Use $\vmeet$ / $\hplus$; $\unk$ absorbs by design.
\end{enumerate}
\begin{remark}[Unknown vs.\ REQUIRED]
$\unk$ represents epistemic uncertainty at the \emph{metric} level; schema failure is a \emph{contract} violation handled before grading.
\end{remark}

\section{The Logic of Obligations ($\Sig$): A Typed Effect System}
\label{sec:sigma}

\subsection{Purpose and Intuition}
Numerical grades in $\Q$ cannot express \emph{stateful contracts}: preconditions, authorizations, quarantines, expiries, and overrides. The effect system $\Sig$ makes these contracts \emph{first-class}, auditable, and compositional. Stages \EF$\!\to$\DRO$\!\to$\PR$\!\to$\CBF$\!\to$\om{} may \emph{emit} obligations and must \emph{discharge} those already active according to explicit rules. A run \emph{passes} only if the final set is empty.

\subsection{Syntax: Types, Scopes, and Structure}
An obligation is a record
\[
  o \;=\; (\textit{tag}:\tau,\; \textit{scope}:s,\; \textit{origin\_id}:u,\; \textit{meta}:m),
\]
where:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item $\tau \in \Sigma_{\text{types}}$ is the obligation \emph{type} (e.g., \code{RequiresHitmap}, \code{OmegaPrecond}, \code{Quarantine}, \code{RetuneDisabledInRed}, \code{RequiresDepCert(CVaR)}, \code{OverrideActive}).
  \item $s \in \{\textit{Stage},\textit{Window},\textit{Global}\}$ controls lifetime; $s$ bounds where discharge may occur.
  \item $u$ identifies the emitter (subsystem, stage, or policy module).
  \item $m$ contains typed fields (e.g., \code{hitmap\_id}, \code{valid\_until}, \code{justification\_code}, \code{signature}).
\end{itemize}
We maintain an \emph{active multiset} $\Sigma \in \mathbb{M}(\Sigma_{\text{types}} \times \text{meta})$ per window.

\subsection{Well-Founded Type Order and Multiset Measure}
To prove termination of discharge, we posit a strict, well-founded order on types
\[
  (\Sigma_{\text{types}},\;\succ) \quad\text{with a typical chain}\quad
  \code{OmegaPrecond}\succ \code{RequiresHitmap}\succ \code{RetuneDisabledInRed}\succ \code{OverrideActive}\succ\cdots
\]
reflecting dependency (preconditions must resolve before retune authorizations, etc.). We lift $\succ$ to a multiset order $\succ_M$ on finite multisets (Dershowitz–Manna). This induces a \emph{measure} $\mu:\mathbb{M}(\Sigma_{\text{types}})\to$ well-founded set, strictly decreasing on passing runs.

\begin{invariant}[\ISigma]\label{inv:sigma-progress}
For any adjacent stages in a \emph{passing} window, $\Sigma_{k}\succ_M \Sigma_{k+1}$. Hence, the discharge process terminates in finitely many steps within the window.
\end{invariant}

\begin{lemma}[Progress via multiset ordering]\label{lem:progress}
If each stage either (i) discharges at least one obligation or (ii) emits only obligations strictly smaller (in $\succ$) than those it discharges, then \cref{inv:sigma-progress} holds.
\end{lemma}

\subsection{Total Discharge Relation}
We define a total, decidable predicate
\[
  \code{DischargedBy}(o,\; \textit{stage},\; \textit{witness}) \in \{\textsf{true},\textsf{false}\}
\]
with a \emph{published matrix} serving as ground truth. Examples:

The matrix is part of the \code{proof\_print} schema version; updates are versioned and auditable.

\subsection{Operational Semantics (per stage)}
Let a stage $S$ consume $(\Q_{\text{in}}, \Sigma_{\text{in}})$ and emit $(\Delta\Q_S, \Delta\Sigma^{+}_S, W_S)$ where $W_S$ are canonical witness bytes. The stage transition is
\[
(\Q_{\text{out}}, \Sigma_{\text{out}}) \;=\;
\Big(\;\Q_{\text{in}} \vmeet \Delta\Q_S,\quad
\big(\Sigma_{\text{in}} \uplus \Delta\Sigma^{+}_S\big) \setminus \{\,o \mid \code{DischargedBy}(o,S,W_S)\,\}\;\Big),
\]
subject to scope checks (a Stage-scoped $o$ can only be discharged by its issuer; Window/Global may be discharged by designated stages). Horizontal wiring composes $\Delta\Q_S$ via $\hplus$ across disjoint subsystems.

\subsection{Soundness and Pass/Fail}
\begin{definition}[Verdict] \label{def:verdict}
A window \emph{passes} iff its final grade $\Q_{\mathrm{final}}$ is within policy and $\Sigma_{\mathrm{final}}=\varnothing$. Otherwise it \emph{fails}.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Obligation soundness]\label{lem:soundness}
If a window passes, then $\Sigma_{\mathrm{final}}=\varnothing$. Conversely, any non-empty $\Sigma_{\mathrm{final}}$ forces failure.
\end{lemma}

\subsection{Non-Expansion Policies (Retune and Overrides)}
\paragraph{Retune.} In Red, retune is statically forbidden (\code{RetuneDisabledInRed}); any retune attempt emits \code{RequiresHitmap} and must be \emph{non-expansive} with respect to critical obligations (it may not introduce a new \code{Quarantine}). Successful retune witnesses include \code{valid\_until} and link to \code{hitmap\_id}.

\paragraph{Overrides.} An override is a \emph{typed, signed effect} serialized as its own block; activation emits \code{OverrideActive} (Global). Expiry or revocation discharges it, and any computation under override changes the final hash.

\subsection{Interaction with $\Q$ and \composeev}
$\Sig$ guards \emph{policy-level} choices that alter horizontal semantics (e.g., CVaR aggregation) and the allow-set of vertical actions (e.g., retune). The $\composeev$ mapping collects $\Delta\Q_S$, $\Delta\Sigma^{+}_S$, discharge events, and $W_S$ into ordered stage blocks; its \emph{lax exchange} property (next section) relies on stage monotonicity in $\Q$, non-expansion of critical $\Sig$ types, and clamp ordering.

\subsection{CI Checks (executable invariants)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{\ISigma:} Randomized pipelines satisfy $\Sigma_k \succ_M \Sigma_{k+1}$ for passing runs.
  \item \textbf{Soundness:} \cref{lem:soundness} enforced as the final gate before serializing \code{proof\_print}.
  \item \textbf{Matrix coverage:} For each obligation type $\tau$, tests exist that (i) emit $\tau$, (ii) discharge $\tau$ at each permitted stage, and (iii) confirm non-discharge elsewhere.
\end{itemize}

\section{The Geometry of Composition (\composeev): A Lax Double Functor}
\label{sec:compose-ev}

\subsection{Why a Double Category}
Safety integration mixes two orthogonal compositions: \emph{vertical} (the fixed pipeline \EF$\!\to$\DRO$\!\to$\PR$\!\to$\CBF$\!\to$\om) and \emph{horizontal} (wiring disjoint subsystems). Perfect commutation is not guaranteed; real systems exhibit integration friction. We therefore use a \emph{lax} structure that preserves composition up to the grade order $\geqQ$.

\subsection{Designs and Evidence}
We work with a double category of designs and a category of evidence:

\paragraph{Designs \(\cD\).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em]
  \item \emph{Objects:} Interfaces (typed I/O of subsystems).
  \item \emph{Vertical morphisms:} Pipeline stages drawn from the ordered set \(\{\EF,\DRO,\PR,\CBF,\om\}\); composition is function composition in that order.
  \item \emph{Horizontal morphisms:} Wiring diagrams joining interfaces; disjointness implies no shared state beyond explicitly wired ports.
  \item \emph{2-cells:} Application of a stage to a wired design (a concrete run configuration).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Evidence \(\cE\).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.8em]
  \item \emph{Objects:} Canonical witnesses \code{proof\_print} containing final \(\Q\), the terminal obligation multiset \(\Sigma_{\mathrm{final}}\), and ordered per-stage blocks.
  \item \emph{Morphisms:} Verified transformations (e.g., aggregation of per-subsystem evidence into a system witness).
  \item \emph{2-cells:} Coherence witnesses that record how vertical/horizontal compositions were realized.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Functor.}
\(\composeev:\cD\Rightarrow\cE\) maps designs to evidence with two key properties: (i) vertical composition is mapped using the \emph{vertical monoid} \((\Q,\vmeet)\) and the \(\Sig\) discharge matrix; (ii) horizontal composition uses the \emph{horizontal monoid} \((\Q,\hplus)\) across disjoint subsystems. Preservation is \emph{lax}: equalities become inequalities in $\geqQ$.

\subsection{Side Conditions (Assumptions for Laxity)}
\begin{assumption}[Stage monotonicity]\label{asm:stage-mono}
Each stage \(S\) is monotone in \(\Q\): if \(q_{\mathrm{in}}\geqQ q'_{\mathrm{in}}\) then \(S(q_{\mathrm{in}})\geqQ S(q'_{\mathrm{in}})\). Adapters into \(\Q\) are monotone (\IMono).
\end{assumption}
\begin{assumption}[Clamp ordering and monotonicity]\label{asm:clamp}
Clamp decisions are taken before stages that depend on clamp state; the clamp function is monotone in \(\Q\) (\IClamp).
\end{assumption}
\begin{assumption}[Non-expansive retunes]\label{asm:retune}
\PR{} retunes are \emph{non-expansive} w.r.t.\ critical obligations: they do not emit new \code{Quarantine}-class obligations; in Red, retune is forbidden (\Sig).
\end{assumption}
\begin{assumption}[Horizontal independence]\label{asm:horizontal}
Horizontal composition wires disjoint subsystems; cross-talk occurs only via specified interfaces and is reflected in the chosen \(\hplus\) rule (sum on \cvar/\ledger, max elsewhere), with deviations gated by \Sig{} certificates.
\end{assumption}
\begin{assumption}[Validation order]\label{asm:validation}
Schema validation precedes grading; \(\unk\) arises only from optional metrics (V1–V3 pipeline).
\end{assumption}

\subsection{Lax Exchange (Order-Bounded Integration)}
\begin{theorem}[Lax Exchange Law for Grades]\label{thm:lax-exchange}
Let \(v\) denote the vertical pipeline and \(h\) a horizontal wiring of disjoint subsystems. Under \cref{asm:stage-mono,asm:clamp,asm:retune,asm:horizontal,asm:validation},
\[
  \mathrm{grade}\big(\composeev(v\circ h)\big)\;\geqQ\;
  \mathrm{grade}\big(\composeev(h\circ v)\big).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Compute grades coordinatewise. Horizontal aggregation uses \((\Q,\hplus)\); vertical uses \((\Q,\vmeet)\). By \cref{prop:lax-interchange}, \((x\vmeet x')\hplus(y\vmeet y') \geqQ (x\hplus y)\vmeet(x'\hplus y')\). Stage monotonicity (\cref{asm:stage-mono}) and clamp ordering (\cref{asm:clamp}) preserve the inequality through the pipeline. Non-expansive retunes (\cref{asm:retune}) prevent new critical coordinates from violating monotonicity. Thus, composing first (then piping) is conservatively no better than piping first (then composing).
\end{proof}

\begin{invariant}[\IExchange]
Randomized integration tests over small circuits satisfy \(\mathrm{grade}(\composeev(v\circ h))\geqQ \mathrm{grade}(\composeev(h\circ v))\).
\end{invariant}

\paragraph{Why \emph{lax} and not strict equality.}
Consider two subsystems \(A,B\) sharing a PR retune. Tuning the \emph{combined} system may slightly improve \prMarginInv{} but worsen \minLBDef{} due to a conservative shared bound; tuning \emph{individually} yields different trade-offs. Because \(\hplus\) mixes sum and max, some coordinates tighten while others loosen, justifying an inequality rather than equality.

\subsection{Operational Orchestration}
Given per-subsystem stage deltas \((\Delta\Q_S^{(u)}, \Delta\Sigma_S^{+(u)}, W_S^{(u)})\), the orchestrator applies:
\[
\textbf{Vertical:}\quad \Q \leftarrow \Q \vmeet \big(\vmeet\nolimits_{S\in\{\EF,\DRO,\PR,\CBF,\om\}} \Delta\Q_S\big),
\qquad
\Sigma \leftarrow \text{discharge}(\Sigma \uplus \Delta\Sigma_S^{+},\, S,\, W_S),
\]
\[
\textbf{Horizontal:}\quad \Delta\Q_S \leftarrow \hplus\nolimits_{u} \Delta\Q_S^{(u)},
\quad
W_S \leftarrow \text{canoncat}\big(W_S^{(u)}\big),
\]
respecting fixed per-stage order, scope rules for \(\Sig\), and canonical concatenation of witness bytes.

\subsection{Evidence Serialization and Hash Coherence}
Each stage emits a domain-tagged block; the final \code{proof\_print} is:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Header:} \code{schema\_version}, \code{hash\_version}, window ID, timestamp, reserved bytes.
  \item \textbf{Blocks (ordered \EF\,\(\to\)\DRO\,\(\to\)\PR\,\(\to\)\CBF\,\(\to\)\om):} \(\langle\)tag, length, canonical witness bytes\(\rangle\).
  \item \textbf{Footer:} Domain-separated BLAKE3-256 over header+blocks.
\end{itemize}
\begin{lemma}[Hash coherence (\IReplay)]\label{lem:hash-coherence}
For reassociation of horizontal wiring with fixed per-stage order, the serialized byte stream is identical; thus the final hash is invariant and runs replay bit-for-bit.
\end{lemma}
\begin{remark}[Determinism envelope]
Bit-level replay requires fixed PRNG seeds, library SHAs, IEEE-754 mode and NaN canonicalization, endianness, canonical ordering of logically unordered data, and (when applicable) WCET traces. These are recorded in the header and/or stage blocks.
\end{remark}

\subsection{What Integrators Get}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{A conservative order policy:} Wiring-first is most conservative; any deviation must be justified (and certified) to improve grades.
  \item \textbf{Local-to-global reasoning:} \((\Q,\vmeet,\hplus)\) and \(\Sig\) make subsystem guarantees compose with an explicit bound.
  \item \textbf{Auditable artifacts:} The evidence trail is deterministic, versioned, and cryptographically sensitive to policy or dependency drift.
\end{itemize}

\section{From Heuristics to Calculus: Migration and Validation}
\label{sec:migration}

\subsection{Guiding Principle: Wrap, Don’t Rewrite}
We migrate by \emph{strangling} legacy guards with thin adapters that present a uniform, auditable interface:
\[
  \textsf{Adapter}_G:\; \text{raw inputs} \;\longrightarrow\;
  \big\langle \textsf{predicate},\; \Delta\Q_G,\; \Delta\Sigma_G^{+},\; W_G \big\rangle,
\]
where $\Delta\Q_G$ is a monotone image into named coordinates (\cref{sec:algebra-Q}), $\Delta\Sigma_G^{+}$ are newly emitted obligations (\cref{sec:sigma}), and $W_G$ are canonical witness bytes. Adapters \emph{must not} leak bespoke scores: they either normalize into $\Q$ or fail the \emph{schema check} (V1).

\subsection{Three Phases}
\paragraph{Phase 1 — Wrap \& Normalize.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(P1.\arabic*)]
  \item Define the \emph{coordinate registry} (names, semantics, normalization map, vertical/horizontal ops, enforcement locus, REQUIRED?).
  \item Implement adapters for each legacy guard $G$ with: (a) schema validation; (b) monotone normalization into $\Delta\Q_G$; (c) canonical $W_G$; (d) \emph{no hidden aggregation}.
  \item Establish CI gates for \IMono{} (adapter monotonicity) and \IMeet{}/\hspace{0pt}\cref{lem:meet-laws,lem:hplus-laws}.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Phase 2 — Install the Calculus.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(P2.\arabic*)]
  \item Provide pure library ops: \code{meetQ} ($\vmeet$), \code{hplusQ} ($\hplus$), \code{seqCompose}, \code{parCompose}, $\Sig$ discharge, and canonical concatenation \code{canoncat}.
  \item Implement $\composeev$ orchestrator with fixed stage order \EF$\!\to$\DRO$\!\to$\PR$\!\to$\CBF$\!\to$\om{}, enforcing assumptions \cref{asm:stage-mono,asm:clamp,asm:retune,asm:horizontal,asm:validation}.
  \item Serialize \code{proof\_print} (header \{\code{schema\_version}, \code{hash\_version}, ids, timestamps, reserved\}; ordered blocks; BLAKE3 footer).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Phase 3 — Shadow, Verify, Cutover.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(P3.\arabic*)]
  \item Run \emph{shadow mode}: legacy decision is authoritative; calculus runs in parallel and logs \code{proof\_print}.
  \item Enforce invariant suite continuously (\cref{sec:invariants}); investigate \emph{any} violation before proceeding.
  \item Cut over when (i) goldens pass; (ii) property tests are stable; (iii) determinism envelope is complete; (iv) governance approvals are recorded as \Sig{} effects.
\end{enumerate}

At that point, the calculus replaces stacking—with proofs and fixtures, not just prose.

\section{Enforceable Governance and Deterministic Audits}
\label{sec:governance-audit}

\subsection{Governance as Typed, Scoped Effects}
Policies are not comments; they are \emph{effects} in $\Sig$ that are emitted, scoped, discharged, and audited.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Overrides as first-class objects.} An override is a signed, typed effect with schema
  \[
    (\textit{justification\_code}:\textsf{Enum},\; \textit{scope}:\{\textit{Stage,Window,Global}\},\; \textit{expiry\_ts}:\mathbb{Z},\; \textit{signature}:\textsf{bytes}).
  \]
  Activation emits \code{OverrideActive} (Global) into $\Sig$; discharge requires \emph{expiry} or \emph{revocation}. The override block is serialized; any run under override yields a distinct final hash.
  \item \textbf{Retune policy (PR).} In Red, retune is statically forbidden (\code{RetuneDisabledInRed}). Any attempt emits \code{RequiresHitmap} and must be \emph{non-expansive} w.r.t.\ critical obligations. A successful witness carries \code{hitmap\_id}, \code{valid\_until}, and a proof of authorization.
  \item \textbf{Risk-composition policy (CVaR).} Non-default horizontal aggregation requires a \Sig{} certificate: \code{IndependenceCert}, \code{MarginalsOnlyCert}, or \code{CopulaCert($\theta$)}. Attempting non-default without a certificate emits \code{RequiresDepCert(CVaR)} and fails at window end if undischarged.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Determinism Envelope (Replayability Contract)}
Bit-for-bit replay (\IReplay) needs a \emph{complete} determinism envelope recorded in the witness:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(D\arabic*)]
  \item \textbf{PRNG seeds} for all randomized components.
  \item \textbf{Software fingerprints:} content hashes of dynamic libraries/toolchain (e.g., BLAS/CUDA/libm, compiler).
  \item \textbf{Floating point:} IEEE-754 mode, denormal handling, and \emph{canonical NaNs}.
  \item \textbf{Data representation:} fixed endianness for all scalars; canonical ordering of logically unordered collections (sets/maps/rows).
  \item \textbf{Concurrency/timing:} fixed schedules or recorded WCET traces for timing-sensitive paths.
  \item \textbf{Environment:} OS/kernel build ID, CPU features bitmap, timezone and clock-source notes for timestamp normalization.
\end{enumerate}
These fields are either in the header or attached as per-stage metadata; omission is a schema error (V1).

\subsection{\code{proof\_print}: Canonical, Hash-Chained Witness}
The evidence stream is a stable byte layout:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Header (fixed order):}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
  \code{schema\_version} : & \code{uint32\_le} \\
  \code{hash\_version}   : & \code{uint32\_le} \\
  \code{window\_id}      : & \code{uint64\_le} \\
  \code{timestamp\_ns}   : & \code{int64\_le} \\
  \code{env\_fingerprints} : & canonical map $\to$ bytes \\
  \code{reserved}        : & zero-filled bytes \\
  \end{tabular}
  \item \textbf{Per-stage blocks (ordered \EF\,\(\to\)\DRO\,\(\to\)\PR\,\(\to\)\CBF\,\(\to\)\om):} 
  \(\langle\)ASCII tag (4), length \code{uint32\_le}, canonical witness bytes\(\rangle\).
  \item \textbf{Footer:} domain-separated BLAKE3-256 over header+blocks (little-endian digest).
\end{itemize}

\begin{lemma}[Replay and naturality]
With fixed per-stage order and canonical concatenation, horizontal reassociation yields identical bytes; the final hash is invariant. Thus \IReplay{} holds and hash serves as a natural transformation over \composeev.
\end{lemma}

\subsection{Threat Model and Trust Boundaries}
\paragraph{Trusted Computing Base (TCB).} Core calculus library (pure $\Q$/$\Sig$ ops, $\composeev$ orchestrator), compiler/toolchain used to build it, OS kernel, and hardware.

\paragraph{Untrusted components.} Legacy guards (wrapped via adapters), third-party telemetry, low-level runtime monitors. These cross into the TCB via \emph{schemas} and are normalized into $\Q$; any drift is detected by (i) schema rejection (V1) and (ii) \emph{determinism diffs} (hash changes with identical inputs).

\paragraph{Vendor drift detection.} Unexpected hash deltas trigger an investigation: compare env fingerprints (D2), floats (D3), ordering (D4), and timing (D5). A new golden may be minted \emph{only} through signed governance recorded as a \Sig{} effect.

\subsection{Audit Workflow (Runbook)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(A\arabic*)]
  \item \textbf{Acquire witness:} collect \code{proof\_print} bytes for the window.
  \item \textbf{Verify integrity:} recompute footer hash; check \code{schema\_version}/\code{hash\_version}.
  \item \textbf{Decode:} parse header, per-stage blocks; reconstruct $(\Q,\Sig)$ via replay.
  \item \textbf{Check verdict:} enforce \cref{def:verdict}; assert $\Sigma_{\mathrm{final}}=\varnothing$ on Pass.
  \item \textbf{Cross-run comparison:} for claimed identical inputs, assert byte-for-byte equality; discrepancies are determinism events, not hand-waved.
  \item \textbf{Override path:} if \code{OverrideActive} present, verify justification, scope, expiry, and that the final hash changed accordingly.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Security Considerations}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Domain separation:} stage tags and context strings are included in the hash preimage to prevent cross-protocol collisions.
  \item \textbf{Keyed attestations:} optional MAC/signature over the final hash for external provenance; keys rotated under governance and recorded in the header.
  \item \textbf{Forward-compatibility:} \code{schema\_version} and reserved bytes permit evolution without ambiguity; upgrades require new goldens.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Operations Playbook (When Things Break)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Invariant breach:} CI blocks promotion; open an incident; only a signed, time-scoped override unblocks (and marks the hash).
  \item \textbf{Schema failure:} immediate reject (V1); no $\Q$ formed; root-cause in adapter or provider.
  \item \textbf{Replay mismatch:} treat as supply-chain or environment drift; diff env fingerprints and floating-point settings; rebaseline via governance if justified.
  \item \textbf{CVaR policy dispute:} require a \Sig{} certificate; otherwise revert to default sum and fail if risk exceeds floor.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What Auditors Get}
A single, deterministic artifact that (i) encodes policy choices as effects, (ii) captures all sources of non-determinism, and (iii) composes predictably via \composeev. The result is \emph{governance by construction}: every exceptional path is serialized, signed, and replayable.

\section{Generalizations \& Scrutiny}
\label{sec:generalizations}

\subsection{Algebraic Strengthening}
\paragraph{Complete lattice and fixpoints.}
Because each $Q_i=\RR_{\ge0}\cup\{\unk\}$ is a complete lattice (with $0$ as bottom and $\unk$ as top) and products preserve completeness, $(\Q,\geqQ)$ is a complete lattice. Thus any monotone $F:\Q\to\Q$ admits a least fixpoint by Tarski; if stages are Scott-continuous (max/$+$ are), the Kleene chain from $\mathbf{0}$ converges to it. This supports \emph{feedback} or \emph{iterative} monitors with principled convergence criteria.

\paragraph{Quantale/rig viewpoint.}
$(\Q,\vmeet,\hplus)$ is an idempotent commutative rig-like algebra on a poset. Making $(\Q,\vmeet)$ a (sup-)semilattice and requiring $\hplus$ to be \emph{residuated} would enable backward reasoning (\emph{what slack suffices to achieve a target bound?}) via right/left residuals, useful for \emph{budget allocation} across subsystems.

\paragraph{T-(co)norms as horizontal families.}
Beyond $\max$/$+$, horizontal combination can be parameterized by \emph{t}-norm/\emph{t}-conorm families (e.g., probabilistic sum, bounded sum), each gated by a \Sig{} certificate and tracked in the witness; the interchange proof generalizes by substituting their known subdistributivity laws.

\subsection{Abstract Interpretation Link}
Normalization maps $g:\text{raw}\to\Q$ form a Galois insertion with a concrete metric domain when there is a pair $(\alpha,\gamma)$ such that $\alpha$ abstracts raw metrics to $\Q$ and $\gamma$ concretizes grades to safe raw regions. This yields \emph{soundness by construction} proofs for adapters and a route to \emph{best correct} normalizations.

\subsection{Effects as Graded Monads}
$\Sig$ can be modeled as a \emph{graded monad} (or effect algebra) indexed by obligation types, with bind laws enforcing scope and discharge order. The multiset measure then appears as a termination metric for graded binds, aligning with effectful semantics in typed PL.

\subsection{Temporal Logic Interface}
The discharge matrix can be viewed as a realizability layer for LTL/MTL obligations (examples already sketched). This suggests a compiler from policy-in-temporal-logic to $\Sig$ artifacts + tests, ensuring \emph{declarative policies} are enforced at runtime.

\subsection{Scrutiny: Conservatism vs.\ Throughput}
The lax exchange bound and default CVaR sum are intentionally conservative; they may reduce throughput. The calculus makes such pessimism \emph{explicit and reversible}: add a dependence certificate to weaken horizontal sums; or refine the coordinate registry to separate truly shared from independent tails.

\subsection{Non-expansive retunes: a metric view}
Equipping $\Q$ with the sup-seminorm $d(x,y)=\sup_i|x_i-y_i|$ (treating $\unk$ as $+\infty$) gives a \emph{non-expansive} requirement $\mathrm{Lip}(\mathrm{PR})\le1$ on safe coordinates and a \emph{no-creation} rule on critical $\Sig$ types. This provides a tunable quantitative contract for PR implementations.

\section{Conclusion: A Principled Path to Composable Safety}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\subsection{What We Have Replaced and With What}
We replaced brittle, order-sensitive \emph{heuristic stacking} with a compact calculus grounded in:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(C\arabic*)]
  \item the \textbf{bimonoidal product poset} \(\Q\) with vertical \(\vmeet\), horizontal \(\hplus\), and a \emph{lax interchange} law (\cref{sec:algebra-Q});
  \item the \textbf{typed effect system} \(\Sig\) with a total \code{DischargedBy} relation and a well-founded multiset measure ensuring progress (\cref{sec:sigma});
  \item the \textbf{lax double functor} $\composeev$ that maps designs to canonical evidence with an order-bounded exchange theorem (\cref{sec:compose-ev}).
\end{enumerate}
Together with a strict validation order and a canonical \code{proof\_print}, the system yields deterministic audits and \emph{governance by construction} (\cref{sec:migration,sec:governance-audit}).

\subsection{Engineering Guarantees (at a Glance)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Determinism:} Replays are bit-for-bit identical (\IReplay).
  \item \textbf{Monotonicity:} Tightening any guard cannot improve grades (\IMono).
  \item \textbf{Composition:} Integration order is conservatively bounded (\IExchange); horizontal sums apply only to \ledger/\cvar.
  \item \textbf{Sound governance:} Retunes/overrides are typed, scoped, signed effects; Pass \(\Rightarrow \Sigma_{\mathrm{final}}=\varnothing\).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Limitations and Scope}
This calculus constrains \emph{aggregation} and \emph{governance}; it does not certify model correctness or sensor fidelity beyond schemas and determinism. The lax exchange bound is intentionally conservative when \(\hplus\) uses sums; certified dependence models can narrow this gap via \(\Sig\).

\subsection{What Ships: A Ready-to-Run Checklist}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=(S\arabic*)]
  \item Coordinate registry filled (names, normalization, vert./horiz.\ ops, enforcement locus, REQUIRED?).
  \item Adapters implemented; \(\Q\) ops (\code{meetQ}, \code{hplusQ}) and \(\Sig\) discharge matrix complete.
  \item $\composeev$ orchestrator with fixed stage order; \code{proof\_print} schema + determinism envelope fields.
  \item Goldens pass; property tests green for algebra, \(\Sig\) soundness, clamp monotonicity, replay.
  \item Governance approvals serialized as \(\Sig\) effects; drift monitoring (determinism diffs) enabled.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Future Work}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item \textbf{Tighten risk composition:} Broaden certified dependence models for \cvar{}; add conformance tests and calibration harnesses.
  \item \textbf{Static typing of pipelines:} A lightweight type system that rejects misordered clamps/retunes at compile-time.
  \item \textbf{Mechanized proofs:} Encode the algebra and \(\Sig\) progress in a proof assistant; auto-generate CI property tests from proofs.
  \item \textbf{Operational tooling:} One-click witness diffing; determinism-drift explainer (library/FP-mode deltas).
  \item \textbf{Reference corpus:} Public goldens covering each invariant and common failure modes for community regression.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Takeaway}
A small set of enforceable laws beats an ever-growing stack of heuristics. By centering \(\Q\), \(\Sig\), and $\composeev$, we obtain a system whose safety is \emph{composable}, whose policies are \emph{enforceable}, and whose audits are \emph{deterministic}. This is a foundation that scales—safely—by construction.

\section{Proofs and Lemmas (Pack)}
\label{app:proofs}

This appendix collects concise proofs and proof sketches for the algebraic, logical, and audit properties used in the main text. Throughout, $\Q=\prod_{i\in I}(\RR_{\ge 0}\cup\{\unk\})$ with coordinatewise order $\geqQ$ (\emph{bigger is worse}), vertical operator $\vmeet$ (max), horizontal operator $\hplus$ (sum on \ledger/\cvar, max elsewhere), and $\unk$ absorbing.

\subsection{Lax Interchange Law}
\begin{proposition}[Lax interchange; cf.\ \cref{prop:lax-interchange}]
\label{prop:app-lax}
For all $x,x',y,y'\in\Q$,
\[
  (x\vmeet x') \hplus (y\vmeet y') \;\geqQ\; (x\hplus y)\vmeet(x'\hplus y').
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Coordinatewise. If $i\notin\{\ledger,\cvar\}$, both sides equal $\max(x_i,x'_i,y_i,y'_i)$. If $i\in\{\ledger,\cvar\}$, the claim reduces to
\[
  \max(x_i,x'_i) + \max(y_i,y'_i) \;\ge\; \max(x_i+y_i,\,x'_i+y'_i),
\]
which holds by case analysis on which terms realize the maxima. Any occurrence of $\unk$ makes both sides $\unk$ by absorption, preserving $\geqQ$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Vertical \& Horizontal Aggregation Laws}
\begin{proposition}[Vertical monoid laws; cf.\ \cref{lem:meet-laws}]
\label{prop:app-vertical}
$(\Q,\vmeet)$ is a commutative, idempotent monoid with identity $0$ (all zeros) and $\unk$ absorbing.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Coordinatewise from $\max$: associativity, commutativity, idempotence are standard; $\max(a,0)=a$ for $a\ge0$ gives identity; $\max(\unk,a)=\unk$ defines absorption.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Horizontal monoid laws; cf.\ \cref{lem:hplus-laws}]
\label{prop:app-horizontal}
$(\Q,\hplus)$ is a commutative monoid with identity $0$ and $\unk$ absorbing.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
On \ledger/\cvar, $\hplus$ is $+$; elsewhere, it is $\max$. Both are associative/commutative with identity $0$. Absorption follows from $a+\unk=\unk$ and $\max(a,\unk)=\unk$ by convention.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Monotonicity of Stages and Adapters}
\begin{lemma}[Monotone adapters/stages; \IMono]
\label{lem:app-mono}
If each adapter map $g$ into designated coordinates of $\Q$ is monotone and stages combine grades only via $\vmeet$ and $\hplus$, then the end-to-end map is monotone: $q_{\mathrm{in}}\geqQ q'_{\mathrm{in}} \Rightarrow q_{\mathrm{out}}\geqQ q'_{\mathrm{out}}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Composition of monotone maps is monotone; $\vmeet$ and $\hplus$ are monotone in each argument (both $\max$ and $+$ are monotone).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Validation-Order Separation}
\begin{lemma}[Schema failure vs.\ $\unk$]
\label{lem:app-validation}
With (V1) schema check $\to$ (V2) form $\Q$ $\to$ (V3) aggregate, REQUIRED-field violations halt \emph{pre}-grading, while missing optional metrics become $\unk$ in $\Q$ and absorb under aggregation. These two failure modes cannot be conflated.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By construction: the $\Q$ vector is not instantiated if (V1) fails; otherwise optional omissions are mapped to $\unk$, and absorption ensures fail-closed propagation distinct from hard rejection.
\end{proof}

\subsection{$\Sig$ Progress via Multiset Ordering}
\begin{proposition}[\ISigma: termination of discharge]
\label{prop:app-sigma}
Let $(\Sigma_{\text{types}},\succ)$ be well-founded and lift to the Dershowitz–Manna multiset order $\succ_M$. If each stage either discharges at least one obligation or emits only obligations strictly smaller (in $\succ$) than those it discharges, then for a passing window $\Sigma_k \succ_M \Sigma_{k+1}$ and the process terminates.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Definitionally matches the multiset step relation: removing a nonempty multiset and replacing by elements each $\,\prec\,$ some removed element yields a strict $\succ_M$ decrease. Well-foundedness precludes infinite descent.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Clamp Monotonicity}
\begin{lemma}[\IClamp]
\label{lem:app-clamp}
If clamp states are determined by fixed thresholds on coordinates of $\Q$ (Green $\prec$ Amber $\prec$ Red), then $q'\geqQ q \Rightarrow \chi(q') \succeq \chi(q)$. Applying clamps earlier in a worsening sequence cannot yield a better final grade.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Threshold monotonicity gives state monotonicity. Early clamp decisions based on no-worse inputs cannot produce less conservative actions; downstream aggregation via $\vmeet/\hplus$ preserves no-better-than.
\end{proof}

\subsection{CVaR Composition Policy}
\begin{proposition}[Default sum is a coherent upper bound]
\label{prop:app-cvar}
For tail-risk coordinate \cvar, $\,\cvar(A\oplus B)=\cvar(A)+\cvar(B)$ implements the Fréchet upper bound (comonotone case). Alternative rules (convolution/copula) produce values $\le$ this bound when their certificates are valid.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
CVaR is coherent and subadditive; equality holds under comonotonicity. Certified independence yields convolution and typically strict inequality; copula models interpolate within Fréchet bounds.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Hash Coherence / Replay}
\begin{lemma}[\IReplay; naturality of hashing]
\label{lem:app-replay}
With fixed per-stage order and canonical concatenation (including canonical ordering of logically unordered data), reassociation of horizontal wiring produces identical serialized bytes; hence the final hash is invariant and bit-for-bit replay reconstructs $(\Q,\Sig)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Associativity of $\hplus$ ensures the same multiset of per-subsystem stage deltas; canonical concatenation removes ordering degrees of freedom; thus the byte stream is identical and the hash equal.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exchange for \composeev}
\begin{theorem}[Lax exchange for evidence; cf.\ \cref{thm:lax-exchange}]
\label{thm:app-exchange}
Under stage monotonicity, clamp ordering, non-expansive retunes, validation order, and \cref{prop:app-lax}, we have
\[
  \mathrm{grade}\big(\composeev(v\circ h)\big)\;\geqQ\;
  \mathrm{grade}\big(\composeev(h\circ v)\big).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Lift \cref{prop:app-lax} through stage monotonicity and clamp monotonicity; non-expansion ensures $\Sig$ does not introduce new critical obligations that could invert the order; serialization preserves the inequality into evidence.
\end{proof}
