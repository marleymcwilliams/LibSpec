\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion1}
% ============================================================

We have developed a framework for modeling decision-objective dynamics using bigraded multivectors, cumulant expansions, and higher-level algebraic structures. Several key insights emerge:

\subsection*{11.1 \quad Synthesis of Theory}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The hybrid exterior algebra formalizes independent and mixed dynamics between decision states and structural environments (G).
    \item Mixed cumulants $\kappa_{p,g}$ serve as rigorous markers of irreducible coupling, grounded in stochastic semantics.
    \item Closure can be addressed in two complementary ways: entropy-regularized variational truncation and Hopf-algebraic coproduct decompositions.
    \item Category theory elevates the framework, ensuring compositional semantics and cumulant-preserving functors.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{11.2 \quad Path to Computation}
\begin{itemize}
    \item A feasible computational roadmap exists: order-3 cumulant estimation with shrinkage and sketching; symbolic derivations with Python libraries; high-performance kernels in C++.
    \item Statistical challenges are mitigated by bootstrap confidence intervals, truncation, and Hopf-projected residuals.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{11.3 \quad Applications and Benchmarks}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Robotics: projected control improves safety/efficiency under changing constraints.
    \item Neuroscience: $\|K_{\text{mix}}\|$ predicts generalization in context-remapping tasks.
    \item Finance: $\kappa_{1,2}$ offers early warning of regime shifts.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{11.4 \quad Validation Roadmap}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Local validity: prove and test the entropy-cumulant inequality; demonstrate stable projected control in one domain.
    \item Cross-domain transfer: validate cumulant-based diagnostics in at least two domains.
    \item Compositionality: demonstrate categorical functors with empirical and theoretical guarantees across interacting systems.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{11.5 \quad Vision and Future Work}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The long-term goal is to unify geometry, information theory, algebraic combinatorics, and control into a coherent methodology.
    \item By coupling rigorous semantics, computational feasibility, and benchmark-driven validation, the joint strategic geometry framework aspires to become a principled tool for designing and governing complex adaptive systems.
\end{itemize}
