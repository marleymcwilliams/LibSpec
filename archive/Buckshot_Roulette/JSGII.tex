\begin{abstract}
Part~II converts the Joint Strategic Geometry program into a \emph{proof-carrying governor}. Every runtime claim about coupling, range, and safety is falsifiable via logged witnesses: (i) KKT traces and dual gaps for soft-transport DRO; (ii) GKYP/LMI feasibility certificates (band endpoints, margin $\constref{epsilon}$, uncertainty set, dual multipliers) for PR; (iii) $\omega$-automata accepting/rejecting runs for LTL specs; and (iv) a hash-chained Provenance Ladder. Two technical closures make the geometry operational: a \emph{saltation--defect} bound parameterized by $(\constref{kappamax},\constref{thetamin},\constref{rtube})$ that `spends' the frontier-defect budget inside the ISS supply rate and sharpens the Decision-Range Conservativity bound; and a \emph{holonomy budget} that limits rotational mass via Davis--Kahan/Wedin plane-drift across crossings.

Sensing is windowed and \emph{identifiable}: Effective Coupling Space $E_t$ is certified by mixed cumulants with FDR control, then stress-tested by \emph{instruments/interventions} on $\Gamma$ or $G$. A \emph{Minimum Transfer Condition (MTC)} promotes a discovery only if it (a) reduces held-out transport error, (b) passes a calibrated SPRT on $M_t$, and (c) preserves Fisher conditioning. The KL--Fisher neighborhood is made \emph{operational} by $r=\varepsilon/\constref{Lpsi}$ with an automatic \emph{leakage clamp} driven by $\constref{chi}(t)$ that shrinks $r$ and freezes new discoveries when conditioning degrades.

Control is a CLF/CBF-QP projected by $\Pi$ with frontier-aware CBF invariance and PR certification on a band; Decision Range $R_t$ is the reported admissible-control volume. A normative dictionary maps legal/fiscal levers (TRO/PI, SLA, anti-commandeering, etc.) to concrete controller contracts. We ship a four-tier artifact (Transport Pack, Provenance Ladder, Coupling ID Sheet with MTC badges, Automata Evidence) and a benchmark suite (negative control, degeneracy stress, formal-spec acceptance). The result is a governor that measurably reduces coupling mass $M_t$, increases decision range $R_t$ subject to I$_5$, and remains auditable end-to-end.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} information geometry; Fisher metric; mixed cumulants; DRO; PR control; CBF/CLF-QP; formal methods; $\omega$-automata; provenance; benchmarks.

\paragraph{Purpose (delta from Part I and from the prior draft).}
This Part~II turns the Part~I geometry into a \emph{proof-carrying governor} with end-to-end, \emph{deterministic, replayable} witnesses. Every claim about coupling ($M_t$), decision range ($R_t$), and safety (I$_1$--I$_5$) is falsifiable via per-window artifacts and acceptance checks. Relative to the previous draft, this revision converts schematic guarantees into \emph{numerical contracts} that auditors can recompute from the Transport Pack + Provenance Ladder alone (no hidden config), and tightens identifiability and control with pre-registered contexts, powered promotions, robust PR certificates, and explicit quarantine/fail-closed policies.

\paragraph{What is new (certificate-first commitments).}
Each item below has a concrete witness and a pass/fail acceptance check in the windowed pipeline.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Numerical saltation contracts \& replay check.} For every frontier tube we now \emph{publish} the evaluated constant $C_{\mathrm{salt}}(\constref{kappamax},\constref{thetamin},\constref{rtube},\hat\nu_{\min},\constref{Smax},\constref{Gmax})$ and ship a \emph{Saltation Calibration} replay that recomputes $\|K{-}I\|$ from logged $(F^\pm,n)$ and asserts $\|K{-}I\|\le C_{\mathrm{salt}}(1{+}0.1)$. Failures raise a Frontier Health alarm. 
\item \textbf{Grazing detection \& dwell modulation.} We log a grazing probability $p_{\mathrm{graze}}$ from angular margins (empirical $\sin\theta$ vs.\ threshold) and \emph{increase} $\constref{taumin}$ inside tubes when $p_{\mathrm{graze}}$ is high; events are counted in the Frontier Health KPI.
\item \textbf{Decision-range conservativity, \emph{instantiated}.} We specify a computable loss function $f\!\left(C_{\mathrm{fr}},\constref{taumin},d(x,\partial\mathcal C)\right)$ (declared in Sec.~\ref{sec:ctrl2}) and export a per-window \emph{residual} $\big(R_t{-}R_t'\big)-f(\cdot)$ with CI; any positive residual yields a rejecting \emph{Conservativity Violation} automata run.
\item \textbf{KL--Fisher radius as a runtime \emph{guard}, not a lemma.} The quadratic proxy is \emph{invoked only if} \texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds} logs $\|\bm\kappa\|_{\FIM^{-1}}\le r=\varepsilon/\widehat{\constref{Lpsi}}$. Else we fail-closed and fall back to nonparametric alarms (HSIC/CVaR). Leakage is handled by a \emph{three-state clamp} (Green/Yellow/Red) with deterministic hysteresis.
\item \textbf{Identifiability with a \emph{Context Registry} \& \emph{Instrument Map}.} Promotions relying on instruments are blocked unless the instrument/context pair is pre-registered and its invariance tests (BY at level $\cutq$) are non-rejecting in the Map.
\item \textbf{MTC with power: \emph{MDE-gated} transfer.} Gate (i) of MTC requires that the DRO improvement $\Delta\hat R_{\mathrm{DRO}}$ not only exclude $0$ but also achieve \emph{power} $\ge 0.8$ for a pre-registered minimal detectable effect $\Delta_{\min}$ (HAC variance). Achieved power is logged on the CIS badge.
\item \textbf{SPRT preconditioned by DRO sensitivity.} Before consuming SPRT outcomes, we check the envelope identity $dv/d\rho=\eta^\star$ by a finite-difference slope vs.\ $\eta^\star$ match ($\le5\%$); failure invalidates the SPRT for that window.
\item \textbf{Adaptive $K$ (finite-degree cumulants).} Under clamp+high residual HSIC with stagnant $|E_t|$, we escalate to $K{=}4$ (BY-controlled, budgeted); the active $K$ is recorded in the CIS.
\item \textbf{PR as a \emph{robust} certificate.} GKYP is enforced over an explicit \emph{uncertainty set} (polytopic vertices or LFT). We export band $(\omega_\ell,\omega_u)$, margin $\constref{epsilon}$, $(P,Q)$, vertex/LFT ids, and transcript id.
\item \textbf{Rights-plane tail protection.} Frontier slack $\sigma_i$ is guarded by a \emph{CVaR}$_{0.95}$ budget; if exceeded, we escalate to freeze+dwell regardless of PR margin.
\item \textbf{Lever selection as a \emph{safe UCB} bandit.} We replace heuristics with a conservative UCB over levers, hard-constrained by QP safety (\#CBF violations $=0$). We log posteriors/CI per lever and the chosen action.
\item \textbf{Deterministic Replay Pack \& automata re-monitor.} We ship a pinned container (image SHA), a single replay script, and hashes of consumed KPI rows; a \emph{re-monitor} reproduces automata verdicts exactly from the Provenance Ladder. Any mismatch is a hard failure.
\item \textbf{New KPIs.} (i) \emph{Coupling Regret} $M_t-M_t^\circ$ vs.\ a decoupled baseline (HAC CI); (ii) \emph{Holonomy per crossing} $\mathrm{Hol}/N_{\mathrm{cross}}$; (iii) \emph{Provenance latency}. A \emph{dual-gap trend} panel alarms if the DRO dual gap plateaus $>10^{-4}$ for $m$ windows.
\item \textbf{Clamp-policy FDR audit.} Weekly null-stream audits estimate realized FDR; if it exceeds $0.9\cutq$, we tighten to $\cutq\leftarrow 0.9\cutq$ and log “Clamp Policy Evidence.”
\item \textbf{Frontier Spend Ledger with CIs (fix).} Per crossing we log $C_{\mathrm{salt}}$ constituents $(\hat\nu_{\min},c_\Sigma,\kappa_{\max},\constref{rtube})$ with \emph{MBB CIs} and charge the ISS \emph{frontier-spend} entry; the ledger feeds the Frontier Health panel and I$_4$ accounting.
\item \textbf{Gap-calibrated plane switching (fix).} Plane updates are \emph{blocked} when the rank-$k$ gap CI for $\Delta\sigma$ contains $0$; the system either escalates $k$ or engages mixed-plane control with a switching-stability witness (CQLF or dwell).
\item \textbf{Rights-plane residual audit at crossings (fix).} We compute a one-sided CI for $h_i^+-h_i^-+\sigma_i$ per crossing; a negative lower bound triggers a \emph{hard} tube failure and a rejecting automaton run with evidence.
\item \textbf{IV Badge (fix).} CIS badges now include (i) first-stage relevance with HAC CI and $F\!>\!10$, (ii) BY-vetted exclusion across contexts, and (iii) an observed \emph{drop} in the target $\kappa_{p,q}^{(m)}$ under instrument ablation/micro-intervention.
\item \textbf{Window quarantine (fix).} If the envelope precondition $dv/d\rho{=}\eta^\star$ fails, the entire window is \emph{quarantined}: no promotions, no model updates, no FDR accounting—monitoring only. Quarantine status and cause are logged in the Ladder.
\item \textbf{Audit feedback as a state machine (fix).} When realized FDR $>\!0.9\cutq$, we deterministically (i) shrink $r\!\leftarrow\!\gamma_r r$, (ii) tighten $\cutq\!\leftarrow\!0.9\cutq$, and (iii) \emph{freeze} promotions for $m$ windows; all steps are artifacted.
\item \textbf{PR robustness hitmap \& band retuning (fix).} We export a vertex/extremal \emph{hitmap} showing margin bottlenecks and apply an automatic \emph{band-shrink} rule when sampled margins drift toward $\constref{epsilon}$.
\item \textbf{Provenance coverage KPI (fix).} Coverage $=$ reproducible\_rows / total\_rows is tracked with alarms; coverage drops trigger clamp tightening and are recorded as policy evidence.
\item \textbf{Automata $\rightarrow$ KPI row links (fix).} Every rejecting run includes witness traces \emph{and} the exact KPI row hashes it consumed; any mismatch is surfaced in CIS and treated as a hard failure.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Core objects (operational recap).}
On each window $[t-\constref{tau},t]$ we compute
\[
M_t=\bm\kappa_t^\top \FIM_t^{-1}\bm\kappa_t,\qquad
E_t=\{(p,q,m): \kappa_{p,q}^{(m)}\neq 0\ \text{post-BY at level }\cutq\ \text{and MTC-passed}\},

Switching across control planes is protected by a \emph{common-$V$} (if feasible) or a minimum dwell certificate with logged dwell; updates respect the \emph{gap-calibrated} gate.

\section{State Spaces \& Hybrid Calculus: Implementation Notes}\label{sec:calc2}
% ============================================================

\noindent\textbf{State and dynamics.}
The joint state is $x_t=(\gamma_t,g_t)\in X=\Gamma\times G$, where $\Gamma$ is a smooth manifold (decisions) and $G$ a Whitney-stratified pseudomanifold (structure).
Dynamics are Filippov, $\dot x\in \mathcal F(x)$, defined stratum-wise and prolonged across frontiers.
Frontier neighborhoods are equipped with tubes of radius $\constref{rtube}$; on entry, the Dirac projector $\Pi$ is frozen and a dwell $\ge \constref{taumin}$ is enforced.

\begin{constbox}
\textbf{System invariants (I$_1$--I$_5$).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item I$_1$ \emph{No-Zeno}: $N_{\mathrm{cross}}(t_1,t_2)\le (t_2-t_1)/\constref{taumin}$.
\item I$_2$ \emph{Continuity/provenance}: hash-chained \emph{Provenance Ladder}.
\item I$_3$ \emph{Capped hidden coupling}: $\mathrm{HSIC}_t(\Gamma,G)\le B_{\mathrm{HSIC}}$ (post-FDR).
\item I$_4$ \emph{Bounded frontier defects}: $\displaystyle\sum_{\text{cross}\le T}\!\int_{\mathrm{cross}}\|\Delta_{\mathrm{fr}}\|\,dt \le C_{\mathrm{fr}}\,N_{\mathrm{cross}}(0,T)$.
\item I$_5$ \emph{Rights-plane safety}: $x(t)\in \mathcal C=\{x:h_i(x)\ge 0~\forall i\}$, enforced via CBFs.
\end{itemize}
\end{constbox}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Frontier calculus primitives.}
Let $\Sigma$ be a smooth codimension-1 frontier between strata with unit normal $n(x)$ and curvature bounded by $\constref{kappamax}$.
On a transverse crossing at $x^\times\in\Sigma$, with limiting vector fields $F^\pm(x^\times)$ and $\nu(x^\times):=n(x^\times)^\top F^-(x^\times)>0$, the saltation matrix is
\[
K(x^\times) \;=\; I \;+\; \frac{\big(F^+(x^\times)-F^-(x^\times)\big)\,n(x^\times)^\top}{n(x^\times)^\top F^-(x^\times)}.
\]

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{lemma}[No-Zeno (implementation guard)]\label{lem:nozeno-impl}
\emph{\layertag{Rep/Alg/Stoch}}
If (i) frontiers are crossed with principal-angle separation $\ge \constref{thetamin}$, (ii) $\mathcal F$ is one-sided Lipschitz with $\constref{LF}$ on strata, and (iii) a freeze-and-dwell policy enforces $\constref{taumin}$ inside frontier tubes of radius $\constref{rtube}$, then Zeno executions are excluded and
$N_{\mathrm{cross}}(t_1,t_2)\le (t_2-t_1)/\constref{taumin}$.
\begin{proofsketch}
Transversality prevents grazing; one-sided Lipschitz bounds re-entry speed; freeze-and-dwell lower-bounds inter-event time by $\constref{taumin}$.
\end{proofsketch}
\uses{(C1) \constref{rtube}, \constref{thetamin}; (C3) \constref{LF}, \constref{taumin}.}
\end{lemma}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{lemma}[Numeric saltation bound, CIs \& calibration witness]\label{lem:saltation-bound}
\emph{\layertag{Alg/Ctrl}}
For each tube $\mathcal T$ we fit a local chart (logged) with curvature $\kappa_{\max}^\mathcal T\!\le\!\constref{kappamax}$ and record the \emph{minimum normal velocity}
\[
\hat\nu_{\min}^\mathcal T:=\min_{\text{labeled crossings}}\, n^\top F^-\quad\text{with an MBB 95\% CI }[\underline\nu^\mathcal T,\overline\nu^\mathcal T],
\]
and an \emph{estimated normal-variation factor} $c_\Sigma^\mathcal T$ with CI $[\underline c_\Sigma^\mathcal T,\overline c_\Sigma^\mathcal T]$ from the fitted chart.\footnote{Moving-block bootstrap (MBB) uses block length $b$ and $B$ resamples; both are logged (Sec.~\ref{sec:stats2}).}
Then the saltation gain obeys
\[
\|K-I\|\ \le\ \frac{\|F^+-F^-\|}{\nu}\,\bigl(1+c_\Sigma^\mathcal T\,\kappa_{\max}^\mathcal T\,\constref{rtube}\bigr)
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\|K-I\|\ \le\ C_{\mathrm{salt}}^\mathcal T
:= \frac{2(\constref{Smax}+\constref{Gmax})}{\hat\nu_{\min}^\mathcal T}
\bigl(1+c_\Sigma^\mathcal T\,\kappa_{\max}^\mathcal T\,\constref{rtube}\bigr).
\]
\emph{Calibration replay:} using the stored $(F^\pm,n)$, recompute $K$ and assert
\(
\|K-I\|_{\text{obs}}\le \overline C_{\mathrm{salt}}^\mathcal T(1+0.1),
\)
where $\overline C_{\mathrm{salt}}^\mathcal T$ evaluates $C_{\mathrm{salt}}^\mathcal T$ at the conservative bounds $(\underline\nu^\mathcal T,\overline c_\Sigma^\mathcal T)$ and $\kappa_{\max}^\mathcal T$.
Failures raise a Frontier Health alarm, insert a \emph{Frontier Spend Ledger} reject (below), and block conservative claims depending on Lem.~\ref{lem:saltation-bound}.
\begin{proofsketch}
As in the prior draft, with denominator control from transversality and numerator bounded by field contrast; chart-induced normal variation yields the $(1+c_\Sigma^\mathcal T\kappa_{\max}^\mathcal T \constref{rtube})$ factor. CIs are propagated by monotonicity.
\end{proofsketch}
\uses{(C1) \constref{kappamax}, \constref{thetamin}, \constref{rtube}; (C2) \constref{Smax}, \constref{Gmax}.}
\end{lemma}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{lemma}[Grazing-risk detection \& dwell modulation]\label{lem:grazing}
\emph{\layertag{Alg/Ctrl}}
Let $\theta$ be the estimated approach angle w.r.t.\ $n$ on tube entry; define the angular margin statistic $m:=\sin\theta$ and the \emph{grazing probability}
\[
p_{\mathrm{graze}}:=\Pr\{\,m<\sin\anglesep\,\mid\,\text{window data}\,\} \;\;\;(\text{HAC CI}).
\]
If $p_{\mathrm{graze}}>\,p_{\mathrm{thresh}}$ (pre-registered), \emph{increase} $\constref{taumin}\leftarrow(1+\gamma_{\mathrm{dwell}})\constref{taumin}$ for the next $\ell$ crossings in $\mathcal T$ and log the event.
\begin{proofsketch}
Near-tangency ($m\!\downarrow\!0$) increases replay variance of $K$; added dwell enforces separation and improves the denominator margin $n^\top F^-$.
\end{proofsketch}
\uses{(C1) \constref{thetamin}, \constref{rtube}.}
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}[ISS with frontier spending (operational form)]\label{thm:ISS2}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl}}
Under I$_1$--I$_5$ and Lem.~\ref{lem:saltation-bound}, the closed loop is ISS with respect to frontier defects:
\[
V(t)\le \beta\!\left(V(0),t\right)\;+\;\gamma\!\left(\sum_{k\le t/\constref{taumin}}\int_{\mathrm{cross}_k}\!\!\|\Delta_{\mathrm{fr}}\|\,dt\right),
\]
and the Decision-Range Conservativity bound holds with an \emph{explicit} function $f$ (Def.~\ref{def:range-f}) and a \emph{logged residual} $\mathsf{Res}_t:=\big(R_t-R_t'\big)-f(\cdot)\le 0$ (CI).
\begin{proofsketch}
Between crossings use standard CLF/CBF decay; at crossings charge the $V$-jump by Lem.~\ref{lem:saltation-bound}; aggregate using I$_1$.
\end{proofsketch}
\uses{(C3) \constref{taumin}; I$_4$; Lem.~\ref{lem:saltation-bound}.}
\end{theorem}

\begin{definition}[Explicit conservativity function]\label{def:range-f}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl}}
Let $U_t$ be the admissible set (PR margin $\ge\constref{epsilon}$, CBFs, actuator/venue limits).
We report $R_t=\mathrm{vol}_r(U_t)$ or an ellipsoidal proxy from the top $r$ singular radii of $J_{\mathrm{eff}}=\Pi J$.
Define
\[
f\!\left(C_{\mathrm{fr}},\constref{taumin},d(x,\partial\mathcal C)\right)
:=\underbrace{C_{R,\mathrm{fr}}\,C_{\mathrm{fr}}}_{\text{frontier spend}} \;+\;
\underbrace{C_{R,\mathrm{cbf}}\;\frac{h(x)}{\|L_g h(x)\|+\delta}}_{\text{safety tightening}},
\]
with $h$ the active CBF (or min over $i$), $\delta>0$ a small guard, and \emph{logged} constants $C_{R,\mathrm{fr}}$, $C_{R,\mathrm{cbf}}$ derived from the CLF/CBF Lipschitz data and the nominal $U_t$ geometry.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Frontier-aware CBF forward invariance with one-sided CI audit]\label{lem:cbf-frontier}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl}}
Suppose each $h_i$ is $C^1$ on strata with relative degree one and Lipschitz gradient on tubes.
If (i) $\Pi$ is frozen inside frontier tubes, (ii) the CBF constraint
$L_f h_i(x)+L_g h_i(x)u \ge -\alpha\,h_i(x)$ is enforced off-tube, and
(iii) on tubes we enforce the relaxed guard
$h_i^+\ge h_i^- - \sigma_i$ with $\sigma_i:=L_{h_i}\,C_{\mathrm{salt}}^\mathcal T\,\|\delta x\|$
(from Lem.~\ref{lem:saltation-bound}),
then $\mathcal C=\{x:h_i(x)\ge 0,\ \forall i\}$ is forward invariant.
\emph{Audit (per crossing):} compute the residual
\(
R_i:=h_i^+ - h_i^- + \sigma_i
\)
and its one-sided (lower) 95\% CI via MBB; if $\underline R_i<0$ for any active $i$, raise a \emph{hard} tube failure and emit a rejecting \textsf{CBF-Residual} automaton run with evidence (resamples, block length, seeds).
\begin{proofsketch}
Standard CBF invariance off-frontier; across frontiers, saltation caps the drop in $h_i$, which the relaxation absorbs. One-sided CI provides statistical margin against measurement noise.
\end{proofsketch}
\uses{(C1) \constref{rtube}; Lem.~\ref{lem:saltation-bound}.}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proposition}[Holonomy budget for control planes]\label{prop:holonomy}
\emph{\layertag{Alg/Ctrl}}
Let $J_{\mathrm{eff}}$ be the effective Jacobian after projection by $\Pi$ and $\mathcal U_k$ the selected $k$-plane.
If $\Delta J$ is the change across a tube and $\delta_{\mathrm{gap}}$ the spectral gap, then
\[
\angle(\mathcal U_k^+,\mathcal U_k^-)
\;\le\; c_{\mathrm{DK}}\frac{\|\Delta J\|}{\delta_{\mathrm{gap}}}
\;\le\; c_{\mathrm{DK}}\frac{C_J\,C_{\mathrm{salt}}^\mathcal T}{\delta_{\mathrm{gap}}},
\]
and the accumulated holonomy satisfies
\(
\mathrm{Hol}(0,T)\le \bar c\, C_{\mathrm{salt}}^\mathcal T\,N_{\mathrm{cross}}(0,T).
\)
We report both $\mathrm{Hol}$ and $\mathrm{Hol}/N_{\mathrm{cross}}$ with bootstrap CIs as KPIs.
\uses{(C1) \constref{kappamax}, \constref{rtube}; Lem.~\ref{lem:saltation-bound}.}
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Switching stability for mixed-plane control]\label{thm:switching}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl}}
When degeneracy triggers mixed-plane control (convex mixture of QPs), stability is certified by either:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item a \emph{common quadratic Lyapunov function (CQLF)} $V=x^\top P x$ with $P\succ0$ satisfying $A_i^\top P+P A_i\prec 0$ for all linearized modes $i$ (witness: LMI feasibility and $P$ hash), or
\item a \emph{minimum dwell} policy: enforce inter-switch dwell $\tau_{\mathrm{sw}}\ge \tau_{\min}^{\mathrm{sw}}$ (logged) that guarantees decay under the measured switch schedule.\footnote{Infeasible CQLF $\Rightarrow$ engage dwell; both are logged as “switching stability witnesses.”}
\end{enumerate}
Any non-frontier switch must respect the recorded $\tau_{\min}^{\mathrm{sw}}$ to comply with I$_1$.
\end{theorem}

\section{Strategic Series and Cross-Cumulants (Operationalized)}\label{sec:stoch2}
% ============================================================

\noindent\textbf{Windowing, whitening, dictionary.}
Fix a window $[t-\constref{\tau},t]$. Build features on $(\Gamma,G)$ and whiten by the windowed Fisher metric $\FIM_t$ computed at the product reference $P_0$ (Def.~\ref{def:ref2}).
Let $\mathcal T_K$ be a finite dictionary of test functionals up to total degree $K\le 3$ (declared and versioned).
Form the mixed cumulant vector $\bm\kappa_t=\big(\kappa_{p,q}^{(m)}\big)$ with $p,q\ge 1$ and define the \emph{Coupling Mass}
\[
M_t:=\bm\kappa_t^\top\FIM_t^{-1}\bm\kappa_t,
\]
and the preliminary \emph{discovery set}
\[
E_t^{\mathrm{raw}}:=\{(p,q,m):\ \text{$\kappa_{p,q}^{(m)}\neq 0$ rejected at level $\cutq$ by BH/BY}\}.
\]
All computations that invoke EF quadratics are restricted to the radius $r=\varepsilon/\constref{Lpsi}$ (Thm.~\ref{thm:FIMbound2}); outside that radius only non-parametric alarms (HSIC, CVaR) are acted upon.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{proposition}[Finite-$K$ factorization test, radius-gated]\label{prop:factK-ops}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch/Inf}}
Within the EF neighborhood $\|\bm\kappa\|_{\FIM^{-1}}\le r$, the test $E_t^{\mathrm{raw}}=\varnothing$ (BH/BY at level $\cutq$) is a level-$\cutq$ certificate of finite-$K$ factorization; moreover $M_t=0$ iff $E_t^{\mathrm{raw}}=\varnothing$.
\begin{proofsketch}
Whitening makes cumulants orthonormal in the $\FIM^{-1}$ norm locally (Thm.~\ref{thm:FIMbound2}); BH/BY controls the FDR for the family $\{\kappa_{p,q}^{(m)}\}$; the vanishing of all mixed cumulants up to $K$ implies finite-$K$ factorization.
\end{proofsketch}
\uses{(C5) \constref{F}, \constref{Lpsi}; (C4) \constref{\tau}.}
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Identifiability layer: instruments and interventions.}
Correlation is not coupling. We attach \emph{instrument} and \emph{intervention} gates to each raw discovery.

\begin{definition}[Instrument \& intervention toggles]\label{def:inst-int}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch}}
An \emph{instrument} $Z$ for a candidate cross-term between $\Gamma$ and $G$ satisfies (i) \emph{relevance}:
$\mathrm{Cov}(Z,\text{cause})\neq 0$; (ii) \emph{exclusion}: $Z$ is conditionally independent of the \emph{effect} given the cause and declared covariates (checked by invariance tests across contexts/tasks).
An \emph{intervention toggle} is a designed, exogenous perturbation to either the $\Gamma$ or $G$ channel that preserves rights-plane constraints and lies within the EF radius.
\end{definition}

% ---- NEW: IV Badge with direction/ablation evidence -------------------------
\begin{definition}[Instrumental-Variable \emph{Badge} (IV-Badge)]\label{def:iv-badge}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch/Inf}}
An instrument $Z$ earns an \textsf{IV-Badge} for a candidate entry $i\!=\!(p,q,m)$ iff all three pass:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Relevance (strength)}: first-stage HAC-robust Wald test and $F$-statistic $>10$; CI excludes $0$.
\item \textbf{Exclusion (context invariance)}: non-rejection across pre-registered contexts under BY at level $\cutq$ for correlations of $Z$ with declared confounders and direct-$\to$effect probes (Context Registry ids logged).
\item \textbf{Direction/ablation evidence}: ablation or micro-intervention that attenuates the specific target $\kappa_{p,q}^{(m)}$ with HAC CI excluding $0$ for the drop $\Delta\kappa_{p,q}^{(m)}<0$ (effect sign logged).
\end{enumerate}
The badge’s transcript (tests, contexts, ablation deltas) is attached to the CIS row for $i$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Operational instrument checks]\label{lem:inst-tests}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch/Inf}}
If (i) relevance holds with $F>10$ and CI excludes $0$, (ii) exclusion holds by BY across contexts, and (iii) the ablation drop on the \emph{target} $\kappa_{p,q}^{(m)}$ is significant with the predicted sign, then the sign of the promoted cross-term is stable under small tilts on the EF ball and ablations reduce the associated $\kappa_{p,q}^{(m)}$ as predicted.
\begin{proofsketch}
Local asymptotics under whitening; BY control over multiple invariance probes; ablation ensures directionality for the specific cumulant component.
\end{proofsketch}
\uses{(C5) \constref{F}, \constref{Lpsi}.}
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Minimum Transfer Condition (MTC) with badges and conditioning.}
A raw discovery must carry predictive value and be robust under drift and conditioning.

\begin{definition}[Minimum Transfer Condition]\label{def:mtc}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch/Clos}}
A candidate index $i\in E_t^{\mathrm{raw}}$ is \emph{promotable} to $E_t$ only if it passes all gates:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Transfer:} adding feature $i$ to the transport map reduces held-out soft-transport loss $\hat R_{\mathrm{DRO}}$ by at least $\Delta_{\min}>0$ (CI excludes $0$; power $\ge 0.8$ for pre-registered MDE).
\item \textbf{Sequential evidence:} an SPRT on $M_t$ with pre-registered $(\alpha,\beta)$ accepts $H_0:\,M_t\le B_{\mathrm{saf}}$ or, if $H_1$ is accepted, the candidate is \emph{rejected} (no promotion).
\item \textbf{Conditioning \& leakage:} Fisher leakage $\constref{chi}(t)$ does not exceed the alarm threshold; else the \emph{leakage clamp} is triggered (below) and promotion is frozen.
\item \textbf{IV-Badge (if instrumented):} any instrument-dependent claim must attach a passing \textsf{IV-Badge} (Def.~\ref{def:iv-badge}); otherwise the entry may be reported as correlational-only and is blocked from actuation.
\end{enumerate}
We write $E_t:=\{i\in E_t^{\mathrm{raw}}:\ \text{MTC+badges passed}\}$ with per-index badges in the CIS.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[FDR safety under MTC+Badge gating]\label{prop:fdr-mtc}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch}}
If the gates are pre-registered and do not use the unadjusted $p$-values themselves (only downstream losses, SPRT outcomes, leakage $\constref{chi}$, and badge transcripts), then the realized FDR on $E_t$ is $\le \cutq$ (conservative) when BY is used for the raw stage.
\begin{proofsketch}
BY controls FDR under arbitrary dependence at the raw stage; post-hoc monotone filters that discard some rejections (MTC+badges) cannot increase FDR.
\end{proofsketch}
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{SPRT from the DRO dual (with precondition).}
Let $\phi_\eta(x;\theta)$ denote the soft-transport dual potential. Define the incremental log-likelihood ratio
\[
\ell_k \;:=\; \phi_{\eta^\star}(x_k;\hat\theta)\;-\;\mathbb E_{H_0}[\phi_{\eta^\star}(X;\hat\theta)],
\qquad
\mathrm{LLR}(n)=\sum_{k=1}^n \ell_k.
\]
With thresholds $A=\log\frac{1-\beta}{\alpha}$ and $B=\log\frac{\beta}{1-\alpha}$, accept $H_1$ if $\mathrm{LLR}\ge A$, $H_0$ if $\mathrm{LLR}\le B$, else continue.
Consumption of SPRT outcomes requires the envelope identity $dv/d\rho=\eta^\star$ to pass within tolerance (Thm.~\ref{thm:rho-sense}).

% ---- NEW: Window quarantine on envelope failure -----------------------------
\begin{definition}[Window quarantine]\label{def:win-quarantine}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch/Clos}}
If the envelope precondition $dv/d\rho=\eta^\star$ fails in window $[t-\constref{\tau},t]$, mark the window \textsf{quarantined}:
(i) \emph{freeze} promotions ($E_t\leftarrow\varnothing$),
(ii) exclude the window from training/updates of adaptive policies and from FDR audits,
(iii) allow only safety monitoring (CBF checks, ISS accounting) and KPI archiving.
The quarantine status (boolean) and reason are recorded in the Provenance Ladder.
\end{definition}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Leakage clamp (automatic radius/claims freeze).}
Define Fisher leakage $\constref{chi}(t):=\|\FIM_t-\FIM_{t-\constref{\tau}}\|/\|\FIM_{t-\constref{\tau}}\|$.
On $\constref{chi}(t)>\chi_{\mathrm{alarm}}$:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item shrink the EF radius $r\leftarrow \gamma_r r$ with $\gamma_r\in(0,1)$, 
\item reduce the discovery level $\cutq\leftarrow \gamma_q\cutq$ (more conservative BY),
\item freeze promotions ($E_t\leftarrow \varnothing$) until $\constref{chi}$ falls below $\chi_{\mathrm{clear}}<\chi_{\mathrm{alarm}}$ for $m$ consecutive windows.
\end{enumerate}
All clamps (trip time, $\gamma_r,\gamma_q$) are logged.

% ---- NEW: Deterministic realized-FDR feedback clamp -------------------------
\paragraph{Realized-FDR feedback (deterministic clamp).}\label{par:fdr-feedback}
Weekly null-stream audits compute $\widehat{\mathrm{FDR}}_{\text{real}}$. If $\widehat{\mathrm{FDR}}_{\text{real}}>0.9\,\cutq$, then deterministically:
\[
\cutq \leftarrow 0.9\,\cutq,\qquad r \leftarrow \gamma_r^{\mathrm{audit}}\, r\ \ (\gamma_r^{\mathrm{audit}}\!\in(0,1)),\qquad
\text{freeze promotions for the next $m_{\mathrm{audit}}$ windows.}
\]
Log a \textsf{Clamp Policy Evidence} record with pre/post $(\cutq,r)$ and audit evidence. Unfreeze only after the next passing audit.

\section{DRO Closure: Tuning, KKT Logging, and Sensitivity}\label{sec:closure2}

\noindent\textbf{Setup (windowed, radius-gated).}
Fix a window $[t-\constref{tau},t]$ with empirical law $\hat P_t$ sourced from the Provenance Ladder.
Let $\ell_\theta(x)$ be convex in $\theta$ and measurable in $x$.
We define the \emph{soft-transport} DRO risk at radius $\constref{rho}$ using either a KL ball or a Wasserstein-$c$ ball; both are handled by a unified, \emph{smooth} dual.
Whenever quadratic EF proxies are used downstream, we require a passing guard \texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds} (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}).

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Primal (fixed $\theta$).}
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{DRO}}(\theta;\constref{rho})
~:=~
\sup_{Q\in\mathcal P}~\Big\{~\mathbb E_Q[\ell_\theta(X)]:~\mathbf D(Q\|\hat P_t)\le \constref{rho}~\Big\},
\]
where $\mathbf D$ is either $(i)$ KL or $(ii)$ Wasserstein-$c$ with continuous, $1$-homogeneous cost $c$ on the EF ball.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Unified dual with deterministic smoothing.}
\textbf{KL case:}\quad
$\displaystyle \mathcal R_{\mathrm{DRO}}
=\inf_{\eta>0}\Big\{\eta\,\constref{rho}+\frac{1}{\eta}\log \mathbb E_{\hat P_t}\!\big[e^{\eta\,\ell_\theta(X)}\big]\Big\}$.

\noindent
\textbf{Wasserstein-$c$ case (smoothed):}\quad
$\displaystyle \mathcal R_{\mathrm{DRO}}
=\inf_{\eta\ge 0}\Big\{\eta\,\constref{rho}+\mathbb E_{\hat P_t}\big[\tilde\phi_\eta(X;\theta)\big]\Big\}$,
with $c$-transform $\phi_\eta(x;\theta){:=}\sup_{y}\{\ell_\theta(y)-\eta\,c(x,y)\}$ replaced by a log-sum-exp smoothing
\[
\tilde\phi_\eta(x;\theta):=\tau_{\mathrm{lse}}\log\!\int\!\exp\!\big(\tfrac{\ell_\theta(y)-\eta c(x,y)}{\tau_{\mathrm{lse}}}\big)\,d\mu(y)
\]
over a compact EF ball, where $\tau_{\mathrm{lse}}\!>\!0$ is chosen so that the \emph{smoothing bias} satisfies
\[
0 \le \mathbb E_{\hat P_t}[\tilde\phi_\eta-\phi_\eta] \le \varepsilon_{\mathrm{lse}}
\qquad\text{(logged, target }\varepsilon_{\mathrm{lse}}\le 10^{-4}\text{)}.
\]
We log $(\tau_{\mathrm{lse}},\varepsilon_{\mathrm{lse}})$ and fail-closed if the bias bound is not met.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{theorem}[Strong duality \& uniqueness of $\constref{etastar}$]\label{thm:duality-uniq}
\emph{\layertag{Clos/Inf}}
Assume: (i) $\ell_\theta$ convex, lsc in $\theta$; (ii) $\mathbf D$ as above with $c$ convex, lsc, coercive; (iii) $\hat P_t$ supported within the EF ball of radius $r$.
Then $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{DRO}}$ equals its dual, the dual objective is strictly convex in $\eta$, and the minimizer $\eta^\star$ is unique.
For the smoothed Wasserstein dual, $\tilde\eta^\star\to\eta^\star$ as $\tau_{\mathrm{lse}}\downarrow 0$ with bias bounded by $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{lse}}$.
\begin{proofsketch}
Fenchel–Rockafellar (KL) and convex conjugacy of $c$-transforms (Wasserstein); strict convexity from log-mgf curvature (KL) or Moreau envelope (smoothed $c$-transform), compactness from radius gating.
\end{proofsketch}
\uses{(C4) \constref{rho}; (C5) \constref{F}, \constref{Lpsi}; (C7) \constref{etastar}.}
\end{theorem}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Tail protection via CVaR (embedded).}
For any KPI stream $Z_t$ (e.g., $M_t$ or slack), embed the Rockafellar–Uryasev epigraph:
$\mathrm{CVaR}_{1-\alpha}(Z)=\min_{\tau}\{\tau+\tfrac{1}{\alpha}\mathbb E[(Z-\tau)_+]\}$,
with multiplier $\lambda_{\mathrm{CVaR}}\!\ge\!0$ in the joint program; log $(\tau^\star,\lambda_{\mathrm{CVaR}}^\star)$.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{proposition}[KKT witness fields]\label{prop:kkt-ops2}
\emph{\layertag{Clos/Inf}}
Under convexity in $\theta$ and strict convexity in $\eta$, the KKT tuple
\[
\left(\eta^\star,\ \lambda_{\mathrm{CVaR}}^\star,\ \tau^\star,\ \text{(band/PR/CBF) multipliers}\right)
\]
exists and is unique in $(\eta^\star,\lambda_{\mathrm{CVaR}}^\star)$.
Per window export: (i) $\eta^\star$ bracket/trace, (ii) dual gap, (iii) CVaR complementary slackness, (iv) AD vs FD gradient RMS, (v) solver transcript id, (vi) $(\tau_{\mathrm{lse}},\varepsilon_{\mathrm{lse}})$ if Wasserstein.
\end{proposition}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{theorem}[$\rho$-sensitivity \& envelope identity]\label{thm:rho-sense}
\emph{\layertag{Clos}}
Let $v(\rho)=\mathcal R_{\mathrm{DRO}}(\theta;\rho)$. Under Thm.~\ref{thm:duality-uniq}, $v$ is differentiable and
\[
\frac{dv}{d\rho}\Big|_{\rho=\constref{rho}}=\eta^\star,
\qquad
\nabla_\theta \mathcal R_{\mathrm{DRO}}(\theta;\rho) = \mathbb E_{\hat P_t}\big[\nabla_\theta \tilde\phi_{\eta^\star}(X;\theta)\big],
\]
with Lipschitz constant controlled by $\eta^\star$ and the EF radius $r$.
We use this identity \emph{as a precondition} for consuming SPRT outcomes (Sec.~\ref{sec:stoch2}) and to gate promotions via the quarantine hook below.
\begin{proofsketch}
Danskin–Envelope theorem with unique $\eta^\star$; interchange of $\nabla_\theta$ and $\mathbb E$ justified by bounded EF ball and Lipschitz $\ell_\theta$.
\end{proofsketch}
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Deterministic replay \& Dual-Gap Trend KPI.}
We pin solver tolerances, seeds, and the evaluation order in a minimal \emph{Replay Pack} (container SHA logged).
Track a \textbf{Dual-Gap Trend} KPI: dual gap $\le 10^{-4}$ (KL) or bias-compensated gap $\le 10^{-4}$ (Wasserstein) and a shrinking $\eta^\star$ bracket. If the dual gap \emph{plateaus above} $10^{-4}$ for $m$ consecutive windows, emit a \textsf{DRO Convergence Alarm} (tuning review of $\rho$, smoothing, or conditioning).

\begin{definition}[Stratified reference $\lambda$]\label{def:ref2}
\emph{\layertag{Rep/Inf}}

Define $\lambda=\lambda_{\cont}+\lambda_{\atom}$ with
$\lambda_{\cont} := \sum_{s} w_s\,\mathcal H^{\dim S_s}\!\!\restriction S_s$ and
$\lambda_{\atom}:=\sum_{p} a_p\,\delta_p$ for isolated singularities.

\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Refinement invariance]\label{lem:refinement}
\emph{\layertag{Rep}}
Under stratified mesh refinements that preserve total weights, $P_0$ and $\FIM_t$ are invariant to first order on the EF ball; $(\FIM_t,W_t,\eta^\star)$ change by $O(r^2)$.
\begin{proofsketch}
Local change-of-variables with Jacobian $\approx I$ on $r$-balls; atoms preserve total weight; Fisher is second-order in cumulants.
\end{proofsketch}
\end{lemma}

\section{Entropy Neighborhood: EF Radius, Conditioning, and Leakage}\label{sec:entropy2}
% ============================================================

\noindent\textbf{Setup (windowed EF neighborhood).}
Work in a window $[t-\constref{tau},t]$ with reference $P_0$ (Def.~\ref{def:ref2}).
Let $\psi(\theta)=\log\mathbb E_{P_0}[e^{\theta^\top T(X)}]$ be the log-partition of the exponential family centered at $P_0$ with sufficient statistics $T$.
Denote the Fisher $\FIM:=\nabla^2\psi(0)$ and the windowed mixed-cumulant vector
$\bm\kappa(\theta):=\nabla\psi(\theta)-\nabla\psi(0)$.
Assume the Hessian is Lipschitz on a neighborhood of $0$:
\[
\|\nabla^2\psi(\theta_1)-\nabla^2\psi(\theta_2)\| \le \constref{Lpsi}\,\|\theta_1-\theta_2\|.
\]

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{theorem}[Local KL--cumulant equivalence \& operational radius]\label{thm:FIMbound2}
\emph{\layertag{Inf}}
Fix $\varepsilon\in(0,1)$ and set the EF radius $r:=\varepsilon/\constref{Lpsi}$.
If $\|\bm\kappa(\theta)\|_{\FIM^{-1}}\le r$, then
\[
\frac{1-\varepsilon}{2}\,\|\bm\kappa(\theta)\|_{\FIM^{-1}}^{2}
\ \le\
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_\theta\|P_0)
\ \le\
\frac{1+\varepsilon}{2}\,\|\bm\kappa(\theta)\|_{\FIM^{-1}}^{2}.
\]
\begin{proofsketch}
Taylor-expand $\psi$ at $0$: $\psi(\theta)=\psi(0)+\tfrac12\theta^\top\FIM\theta+R_3$, with
$\|R_3\|\le \tfrac{\constref{Lpsi}}{6}\|\theta\|^3$ by Hessian-Lipschitzness.
Mean parameters obey $\bm\kappa(\theta)=\FIM\theta+O(\|\theta\|^2)$, hence $\theta=\FIM^{-1}\bm\kappa+O(\|\bm\kappa\|_{\FIM^{-1}}^2)$.
Plugging into $D_{\mathrm{KL}}=\psi(\theta)-\psi(0)-\theta^\top\nabla\psi(0)$ and requiring $\|\bm\kappa\|_{\FIM^{-1}}\le r=\varepsilon/\constref{Lpsi}$ bounds the third-order remainder by an $\varepsilon$-fraction of the quadratic term.
\end{proofsketch}
\uses{(C5) \constref{F}, \constref{Lpsi}.}
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Stability of $M$ and Fisher on the EF ball]\label{cor:radius-stability}
\emph{\layertag{Inf}}
Within $\|\bm\kappa\|_{\FIM^{-1}}\le r$, the coupling mass $M=\|\bm\kappa\|_{\FIM^{-1}}^{2}$ and Fisher vary Lipschitzly:
\[
\big|D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_{\theta_1}\|P_{\theta_2})-\tfrac12\|\bm\kappa_1-\bm\kappa_2\|_{\FIM^{-1}}^2\big|
\le \varepsilon\,\tfrac12\|\bm\kappa_1-\bm\kappa_2\|_{\FIM^{-1}}^2,\qquad
\|\FIM(\theta)-\FIM\|\le \constref{Lpsi}\,\|\theta\|=O(\varepsilon).
\]
\end{corollary}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Runtime guard: proof-use as a contract.}
Any theorem/algorithm that invokes the quadratic EF proxy must \emph{log} a passing call to
\[
\texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds}(\bm\kappa_t,\FIM_t^{-1},\varepsilon)=\textsf{pass}
\quad\text{where}\quad r=\varepsilon/\widehat{\constref{Lpsi}}.
\]
If the guard fails, we \emph{fail-closed}: disable the proxy for that window and act only on nonparametric alarms (HSIC, CVaR); downstream acceptance checks must reference this guard (see \S\ref{sec:stoch2}, \S\ref{sec:closure2}).

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Estimating $\constref{Lpsi}$ (small-tilt with HAC CIs).}
We estimate $\constref{Lpsi}$ directly via finite differences of $\nabla^2\psi$ along an orthonormal basis in the $\FIM$-whitened space.

\begin{definition}[Finite-difference Lipschitz estimator $\widehat{\constref{Lpsi}}$]\label{def:Lpsi-est}
\emph{\layertag{Inf/Comp}}
Let $\{e_j\}_{j=1}^d$ be orthonormal in the whitened space. For a grid $\delta\in\{\pm\delta_0,\pm2\delta_0\}$ (inside the EF ball), estimate
\[
\widehat{\FIM}(\delta e_j)=\nabla^2\psi(\delta e_j)\quad(\text{as score covariance/log-mgf curvature}),
\qquad
\widehat{L}_j:=\max_{\delta_1\neq\delta_2}\frac{\|\widehat{\FIM}(\delta_1 e_j)-\widehat{\FIM}(\delta_2 e_j)\|}{|\delta_1-\delta_2|}.
\]
Let $\widehat{\constref{Lpsi}}:=\max_j\,\mathrm{HAC\text{-}CI\ upper}(\widehat{L}_j)$.
Set the radius $r:=\varepsilon/\widehat{\constref{Lpsi}}$ and \emph{log} $(\varepsilon,\widehat{\constref{Lpsi}},r,\text{CI})$.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Policy \& defaults]\label{rem:eps-policy}
Default $\varepsilon=0.2$; if $\mathrm{width}(\text{CI})/\widehat{\constref{Lpsi}}>0.3$, automatically tighten to $\varepsilon=0.1$ (downgrade logged).
Define Fisher leakage $\constref{chi}(t):=\|\FIM_{t}-\FIM_{t-\constref{tau}}\|/\|\FIM_{t-\constref{tau}}\|$.
\end{remark}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Deterministic leakage clamp (three-state, replayable).}
We operate a state machine on the smoothed leakage $\tilde\chi_t$ (3-window robust mean):
\[
\textsc{Green: } \tilde\chi_t\le \chi_{\mathrm{green}},\quad
\textsc{Yellow: } \chi_{\mathrm{green}}<\tilde\chi_t\le \chi_{\mathrm{alarm}},\quad
\textsc{Red: } \tilde\chi_t>\chi_{\mathrm{alarm}}.
\]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Thresholds (defaults).} $\chi_{\mathrm{green}}{=}0.05$, $\chi_{\mathrm{alarm}}{=}0.15$, clearance $\chi_{\mathrm{clear}}{=}0.08$ (hysteresis).
\item \textbf{Actions.} \textsc{Yellow}: shrink $r\leftarrow \gamma_y r$ and $\cutq\leftarrow \gamma_q\cutq$ (defaults $\gamma_y{=}0.8$, $\gamma_q{=}0.8$); recompute \texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds}. \textsc{Red}: freeze promotions ($E_t\!\leftarrow\!\varnothing$), set $r\leftarrow 0.5r$, and act only on HSIC/CVaR alarms until clearance.
\item \textbf{Unclamp.} Require $\tilde\chi_t<\chi_{\mathrm{clear}}$ for $m{=}3$ consecutive windows, then restore $(r,\cutq)$ linearly over the next $m$ windows (logged).
\item \textbf{Determinism.} Clamp decisions are a pure function of the logged $\tilde\chi_t$ series and the $(\chi_{\mathrm{green}},\chi_{\mathrm{alarm}},\chi_{\mathrm{clear}},\gamma_y,\gamma_q,m)$ tuple; the replay tool recomputes identical states.
\end{itemize}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{definition}[Window Quarantine]\label{def:win-quarantine}
\emph{\layertag{Rep/Stoch}}
A window is \emph{quarantined} if any precondition for EF-based inference fails (e.g., envelope identity $dv/d\rho=\eta^\star$ in \S\ref{sec:closure2}, or the EF guard above).
\textbf{Effect:} For quarantined windows: (i) promotions freeze ($E_t\!=\!\varnothing$), (ii) adaptive updates (bandit/posteriors, PR retuning) are disabled, (iii) safety-only checks (CBFs, PR feasibility, CVaR) continue. The Ladder logs \texttt{quarantine\_status=true} and \texttt{reason}.
\end{definition}

\section{Projected Control: PR Certification and ISS Budgets}\label{sec:ctrl2}
% ============================================================

\noindent\textbf{Controller stack (frontier-aware).}
At each step we solve a CLF/CBF-QP on the active stratum and then project via the Dirac structure $\Pi$; inside frontier tubes of radius $\constref{rtube}$ we \emph{freeze} $\Pi$ and enforce dwell $\ge\constref{taumin}$ (I$_1$), charging the saltation spend to the ISS budget (Lem.~\ref{lem:saltation-bound}, Thm.~\ref{thm:ISS2}). The admissible set is guarded by PR on a band with margin $\constref{epsilon}$.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{CLF/CBF-QP with frontier guards and slack tail control}
Consider $\dot x=f(x)+g(x)u$ (stratum-local). Pick CLF $V$ and CBFs $\{h_i\}$; enumerate assumptions (relative degree, smoothness) in the artifact.

\[
\begin{aligned}
\min_{u,\ \delta\ge0,\ s\ge 0}\quad & \tfrac12\,u^\top R\,u + \alpha\,\delta^2 + \beta\,\|s\|_1 \\
\text{s.t.}\qquad & L_f V(x)+L_g V(x)\,u \le -\lambda_V V(x) + \delta \\
& L_f h_i(x)+L_g h_i(x)\,u \ge -\gamma_i\,h_i(x) - s_i \qquad \text{(off-tube)}\\
& h_i^+ \ge h_i^- - \sigma_i \ \ \text{on tubes},\quad \sigma_i:=L_{h_i}\,C_{\mathrm{salt}}\,\|\delta x\| \ \ (\text{Lemma \ref{lem:cbf-frontier}})\\
& u\in \mathcal U_{\text{act}},\ \ \text{PR margin}(u;\Omega)\ge \constref{epsilon}.
\end{aligned}
\]
We track the \emph{soft-CBF slack stream} $\{s_i\}$ off-tube and the \emph{tube slack} $\{\sigma_i\}$ on-tube.

\paragraph{CVaR guard on safety slack (rights-plane first).}
Define $\mathrm{CVaR}_{1-\alpha}(s_i)$ and $\mathrm{CVaR}_{1-\alpha}(\sigma_i)$ via Rockafellar–Uryasev. Impose budgets
\[
\mathrm{CVaR}_{1-\alpha}(s_i) \le S_{\max,i},\qquad
\mathrm{CVaR}_{1-\alpha}(\sigma_i) \le \Sigma_{\max,i}.
\]
If either budget is violated on the rolling window, \emph{escalate immediately} to freeze+dwell (regardless of PR margin), then re-enter with tightened gains $\gamma_i\uparrow$ and a reduced decision band (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2} clamp). CVaR witnesses are exported per window (Sec.~\ref{sec:closure2}).

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{PR on a band with explicit uncertainty}
Let $(A,B,C,D)$ be the linearization on the active stratum; we certify PR on $\Omega=[\omega_\ell,\omega_u]$ with margin $\constref{epsilon}$ over a declared uncertainty set $\mathcal U$:

\begin{definition}[Uncertainty set for GKYP]\label{def:unc-set}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl/Rep}}
Either (i) \textbf{Polytopic:} $\mathcal U=\mathrm{conv}\{(A_j,B_j,C_j,D_j)\}_{j=1}^J$ from vertices taken at a hashed operating-region grid; or (ii) \textbf{LFT:} $(A,B,C,D)=\mathcal F_\ell(M,\Delta)$ with $\Delta\in\mathbb D$ (structured, norm-bounded). We log vertex IDs or LFT structure, operating-region hash, and sampling grids.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-frequency GKYP PR certificate over $\mathcal U$]\label{thm:gkyp-unc}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl}}
Fix band $\Omega$ and margin $\constref{epsilon}$. If there exist $P\succ0$, $Q\succeq0$ s.t.
\[
\mathcal L(P,Q;A,B,C,D,\Omega,\constref{epsilon}) \ \preceq\ 0
\]
for all $(A,B,C,D)\in\mathcal U$ (all vertices for polytopes; all extremals of $\mathbb D$ for LFT in a standard scaling), where $\mathcal L(\cdot)$ denotes the GKYP LMI with band multiplier, then $G(s)=C(sI-A)^{-1}B+D$ is PR with margin $\constref{epsilon}$ for all models in $\mathcal U$ and all $\omega\in\Omega$.
\begin{proofsketch}
Generalized KYP with finite-frequency multipliers; robust feasibility holds by convexity over vertices or by scaled small-gain/IQC arguments for LFT extremals.
\end{proofsketch}
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Witness export, hitmap, and sample validation]\label{rem:hitmap}
We export $\Omega$, $\constref{epsilon}$, uncertainty descriptor (vertex IDs or LFT hash), $(P,Q)$, dual multipliers, solver transcript. A dense frequency sweep (post-check) is logged with minimum sampled PR margin and a \emph{vertex/LFT hitmap} $\mathsf{Hit}=\{(\text{id}, \min_{\omega\in\Omega}\text{PRmargin}_{\text{id}}(\omega))\}$ highlighting the tight models. The hitmap drives retuning (Def.~\ref{def:band-retune}).
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[Automatic band retuning]\label{def:band-retune}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl/Alg}}
If the sampled minimum PR margin over $\Omega$ falls below $\constref{epsilon}(1+\eta_{\mathrm{band}})$ with $\eta_{\mathrm{band}}\in[0,0.2]$, \emph{shrink} the band by the smallest symmetric factor $\gamma_\Omega\in(0,1)$ such that the post-check minimum sampled margin recovers $\ge \constref{epsilon}(1+\eta_{\mathrm{band}})$; log $(\gamma_\Omega,\eta_{\mathrm{band}})$ and the hitmap. Under clamp \textsc{Red} (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}), retuning is disabled.
\end{definition}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Lever selection as a safe bandit}\label{sec:ctrl2-bandit}
Let $\mathcal L$ be feasible \emph{levers} (venue/policy toggles, PR margin raise, intervention intensity, allocation). Define the (negative) coupling gain and collateral cost:
\[
\mathrm{gain}(\ell) := -\frac{d}{ds}\Big\|\bm\kappa_s\Big\|_{\FIM^{-1}}\!\Big|_{s=0,\ell},\qquad
\mathrm{cost}(\ell) := \lambda_R\,\Delta[-\log R_t] + \lambda_{\$}\,\text{budget}_\ell.
\]

\paragraph{Curvature-per-collateral objective.}
Define the \emph{curvature damping per collateral} index
\[
\mathrm{CPC}(\ell)\ :=\ \frac{\text{ISS decay gain}_\ell}{\mathrm{cost}(\ell)}
\ \approx\
\frac{\lambda_V \Delta V^{\downarrow}_\ell + c_{\mathrm{fr}}\Delta C_{\mathrm{fr}}^{\downarrow} + c_{\mathrm{hol}}\Delta \mathrm{Hol}^{\downarrow}}{\lambda_R\,\Delta[-\log R_t] + \lambda_{\$}\,\text{budget}_\ell},
\]
estimated from small-tilt experiments within the EF ball (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}). The safe-UCB selects
\[
\ell_t = \arg\max_{\ell\in\mathcal L_t}\ \widehat{\mathrm{CPC}}_\ell(t)
+ c\sqrt{\tfrac{\log t}{N_\ell(t)}}
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
\texttt{is\_safe}(\ell)=\textsf{true},
\]
where \texttt{is\_safe} requires: (i) QP feasibility with CBF/PR constraints; (ii) predicted CVaR slack budgets will not be violated; (iii) no clamp upgrade (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}).

\emph{\layertag{Rep/Ctrl}}

\end{definition}

\paragraph{Telemetry.}

\subsection*{Mixed-plane controller: switching stability}
When constrained SVD (Sec.~\ref{sec:kernels2}) flags degeneracy ($\Delta\sigma$ CI contains $0$), we activate a mixed-plane controller (convex combination of QPs) with a formal stability witness:

\begin{theorem}[Common-$V$ or dwell-time certificate]\label{thm:switch-stab}
\emph{\layertag{Ctrl}}
Let $\{A_i,B_i\}$ be linearizations tied to QP choices at the mixture vertices. If there exists $P\succ 0$ with $A_i^\top P+P A_i \prec 0$ for all $i$ (CQLF), then any convex mixing/scheduling is exponentially stable. If no CQLF exists, enforce a minimum dwell-time $\tau_{\mathrm{dwell}}$ between controller switches; with Lipschitz $f,g$ and bounded disturbances on the EF ball, there exists $\tau_{\min}^{\mathrm{switch}}>0$ that guarantees ISS under the observed switch schedule. We log the feasible $P$ (hash) or the enforced $\tau_{\mathrm{dwell}}$.
\begin{proofsketch}
Quadratic common Lyapunov suffices for arbitrary switching; otherwise standard average dwell-time ISS arguments with bounded inter-switch energy.
\end{proofsketch}
\end{theorem}

\section{Computable Kernels: Plane Selection \& Structural Derivatives}\label{sec:kernels2}

\noindent\textbf{Effective map and whitening (radius-gated).}
Let $J_{\mathrm{eff}}:=\Pi J_t$ denote the projected (Dirac) Jacobian; whiten by the window Fisher factor $W_t$ so that
\[
\widetilde J_t := W_t\,J_{\mathrm{eff}}, \qquad \FIM_t=W_t^\top W_t,
\]
and require a passing guard \texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds} (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}) whenever EF quadratics are invoked.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Range proxy and confidence}
Compute $\widetilde J_t=U\Sigma V^\top$ with singular values $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_m$.
For rank $r$, define the decision-range proxy (ellipsoidal volume up to a constant)
\[
\widehat R_t \ :=\ \prod_{i=1}^r \sigma_i,
\qquad
\log \widehat R_t \ =\ \sum_{i=1}^r \log \sigma_i,
\]
and report bootstrap CIs via a moving-block bootstrap (MBB) matched to the window (Sec.~\ref{sec:stats2}).

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Constrained SVD (masks, groups, reproducible)}
Venue/structure constraints are encoded by a binary mask $M\in\{0,1\}^{m\times m}$ (1=allowed) and optional group weights $\{\omega_g\}$.

\begin{definition}[Constrained SVD]\label{def:csvd}
\emph{\layertag{Alg/Ctrl}}
Given $k\!\le\!r$, solve
\[
\max_{V\in\mathbb R^{m\times k}}~ \|\widetilde J_t V\|_F^2
\quad
\text{s.t. } V^\top V=I_k,\ \ V\odot(1{-}M)=0,\ \ \sum_g \omega_g\|V_g\|_F \le B,
\]
and set the selected control plane $\mathcal U_k:=\mathrm{span}(U_{[:,1:k]})$.
We pin a PRNG seed and power iterations $\le 3$; all randomization and tolerances are logged (replayable).
\end{definition}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Degeneracy detection, gap-gated updates, and escalation}
Let the spectral gap at rank $k$ be $\Delta\sigma:=\sigma_k-\sigma_{k+1}$.
We compute a BY-controlled (level $\cutq$) MBB CI for $\Delta\sigma$.

\begin{lemma}[Gap-calibrated update gate \& degeneracy policy]\label{lem:deg}
\emph{\layertag{Alg/Ctrl}}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Gap CI gate.} If the MBB CI for $\Delta\sigma$ contains $0$, \emph{block} basis updates to $\mathcal U_k$ for this window and log the CI (hash). A blocked update must be accompanied by one of:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=10pt]
\item \emph{$k$-escalation:} set $k\leftarrow k{+}1$ and recompute $\mathcal U_k$, or
\item \emph{mixed-plane control:} activate the mixture with a switching-stability witness (Thm.~\ref{thm:switch-stab}).
\end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Holonomy throttle.} While updates are blocked, throttle non-frontier switching to respect the holonomy budget (Prop.~\ref{prop:holonomy}).
\item \textbf{Audit trail.} The Provenance Ladder records: CI endpoints, action taken (escalate/mix), and the resulting $k$ or switching certificate id.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Drift, holonomy, and calibration}
Across a frontier tube, let the whitened Jacobian jump be $\Delta \widetilde J:=\widetilde J^{+}-\widetilde J^{-}$ and the local gap $\delta_{\mathrm{gap}}$ at rank $k$.
Davis–Kahan/Wedin yields
\[
\angle\!\big(\mathcal U_k^+,\mathcal U_k^-\big)
\ \le\
c_{\mathrm{DK}}\frac{\|\Delta \widetilde J\|_2}{\delta_{\mathrm{gap}}},
\qquad c_{\mathrm{DK}}=O(1).
\]
Summing principal-angle increments over crossings defines the \emph{holonomy} $\mathrm{Hol}(0,T)$ and the KPI $\mathrm{Hol}/N_{\mathrm{cross}}$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:stats2}).

\begin{proposition}[Tube-level calibration (replayable)]\label{prop:dk-cal}
\emph{\layertag{Alg}}
For each labeled crossing we log $\big(\widetilde J^{-},\widetilde J^{+},\delta_{\mathrm{gap}},\angle(\mathcal U_k^+,\mathcal U_k^-)\big)$.
Acceptance requires
\[
\angle(\mathcal U_k^+,\mathcal U_k^-)\ \le\ (1+\eta_{\mathrm{cal}})\ c_{\mathrm{DK}}\frac{\|\Delta \widetilde J\|_2}{\delta_{\mathrm{gap}}},\quad \eta_{\mathrm{cal}}=0.1,
\]
else raise a \emph{DK-bound violation} alarm (artifacted). Holonomy accumulation and the normalized KPI are exported with bootstrap CIs.
\end{proposition}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Harmonic/rotational mass KPI (small-gap sensitive)}
To surface small-gap fragility we define a harmonic penalty that upweights rotations occurring where the gap is tight.
Let $\vartheta_j$ be the $j$th principal-angle increment and $\delta_{\mathrm{gap},j}$ the contemporaneous gap; with a floor $\underline\delta>0$,
\[
\mathrm{RotMass}(0,T):=\sum_{j}\vartheta_j,
\qquad
\mathrm{HarmMass}(0,T):=\sum_{j}\frac{\vartheta_j}{\max(\delta_{\mathrm{gap},j},\underline\delta)}.
\]
We report both as KPIs with CIs; rising \emph{HarmMass} with flat $N_{\mathrm{cross}}$ triggers $k$-escalation or mixed-plane control (logged).

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Finite-difference structural derivatives (auto $h^\ast$)}
We estimate structural derivatives of $J_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and singular data using central differences with an adaptive step.

\begin{definition}[Automatic step policy]\label{def:hstar}
\emph{\layertag{Comp}}
With a Lipschitz estimate $L_f$ (flow map) and HAC noise variance $\hat\sigma^2$, pick
\[
h^\ast \ =\ \arg\min_{h\in[h_{\min},h_{\max}]}\ \underbrace{c_1 L_f h}_{\text{truncation}} + \underbrace{c_2\, \hat\sigma\, h^{-1}/\sqrt{N}}_{\text{noise}}
\ \approx\
\sqrt{\frac{c_2\hat\sigma}{c_1 L_f \sqrt{N}}}.
\]
We log $(h^\ast,L_f,\hat\sigma,N)$ and the AD/FD cross-check RMS for diagnostics.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Stable singular and plane derivatives]\label{prop:svdgrads}
\emph{\layertag{Comp}}
With $h^\ast$ and radius gating, FD estimates of $\partial \sigma_i/\partial \theta$ and of small-tilt principal angles have RMS error $O(h^\ast)$ and pass AD/FD within $1\%$ RMS on calibration traces.
\end{proposition}

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Holonomy–range coupling (operational bound)}
Let $\vartheta_j$ be per-crossing principal-angle increments. Then
\[
\log \widehat R_T \ \ge\ \log \widehat R_0 \ -\ c_R \sum_j \vartheta_j,
\qquad
c_R:=\kappa_R\,\frac{\max_{i\le r}\sigma_i}{\min_{i\le r}\sigma_i},
\]
and rising $\mathrm{Hol}/N_{\mathrm{cross}}$ or \emph{HarmMass} with flat $N_{\mathrm{cross}}$ triggers $k$-escalation or lever change (Sec.~\ref{sec:ctrl2}).

\section{Algebraic/Categorical Layer: Quasi-Shuffle \& Lipschitz}\label{sec:adv-structures2}
% ============================================================

\noindent\textbf{Alphabet, words, whitening (radius-gated).}
Let $\mathfrak A=\mathfrak A_\Gamma \sqcup \mathfrak A_G$ be whitened letters (windowed dictionary $\mathcal T_K$, versioned).
A word $w=a_1\cdots a_m$ has degree $|w|=m$ and type $(p,q)$ if it contains $p$ letters from $\mathfrak A_\Gamma$ and $q$ from $\mathfrak A_G$.
Within the EF ball (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}) we work in coordinates whitened by $W_t$ so that the Fisher inner product is
$\langle u,v\rangle_{\FIM^{-1}}=u^\top v$ for letters; we drop the subscript when unambiguous.
Every use of EF quadratics in this section requires a passing guard \texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds} (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}).

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Merge Registry (declared, hashed, replayable)}
We restrict quasi-shuffle \emph{merges} to admissible pairs recorded in a versioned \textsf{MergeRegistry.json}:
\[
(a u)\uplus(b v)=a(u\uplus b v) + b(a u\uplus v) + (a\ast b)(u\uplus v),\qquad 1\uplus w=w\uplus 1=w,
\]
where $(a\ast b)\in\mathfrak A$ is allowed iff $(a,b)\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{adm}}$ (from the registry).
The registry file’s schema (fields: \texttt{letters}, \texttt{pairs}, \texttt{time}, \texttt{hash}) and its content hash are pinned in the Provenance Ladder; promotions depending on a merge not in $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{adm}}$ are \emph{blocked}.

\paragraph{Stratified/clamp-aware gating.}
Cross-frontier merges are disabled inside frontier tubes (respecting $\Pi$-freeze, Sec.~\ref{sec:calc2}) and \emph{all} cross-channel merges are disabled in \textsc{Red} clamp (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}); events are logged with window id and registry hash.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Radius-gated orthonormality and associator budget}
\begin{lemma}[Local orthonormality of letters]\label{lem:ortho}
\emph{\layertag{Alg/Inf}}
On the EF ball $r=\varepsilon/\constref{Lpsi}$, whitened letters obey
\(
|\langle a,b\rangle - \delta_{ab}|\le c_0\,\varepsilon,
\)
with $c_0=O(1)$ determined by dictionary smoothness. Hence multilinear operators built from letters have norms $1+O(\varepsilon)$.
\uses{Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}.}
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}[Associativity up to $O(\varepsilon)$]\label{thm:qshuffle-assoc}
\emph{\layertag{Alg}}
With merges restricted to $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{adm}}$ (degree $K\le 3$),
\[
\big\|(u\uplus v)\uplus w - u\uplus (v\uplus w)\big\|
\ \le\ c_{\mathrm{assoc}}\,\varepsilon\,\|u\|\,\|v\|\,\|w\|
\]
for all words $u,v,w$ of total degree $\le K$, with $c_{\mathrm{assoc}}=O(1)$ depending only on the registry.
\begin{proofsketch}
Exact orthonormal letters give zero associator; Lem.~\ref{lem:ortho} yields $O(\varepsilon)$ deviation; truncation $K\le 3$ bounds remainders.
\end{proofsketch}
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Associator budget KPI.}
Define $\mathrm{Assoc}_t:=\max_{\deg\le3}\|(u\uplus v)\uplus w-u\uplus(v\uplus w)\|$ on a seeded random test basis.
We report $\mathrm{Assoc}_t \le c_{\mathrm{assoc}}\widehat\varepsilon_t$ with $\widehat\varepsilon_t:=\|\bm\kappa_t\|_{\FIM_t^{-1}}/\widehat{\constref{Lpsi}}$; violations raise an alarm and block cross-channel promotions for that window.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Non-expansiveness and factorization stability}
\begin{proposition}[Non-expansiveness of the cumulant map]\label{prop:nonexp}
\emph{\layertag{Alg/Inf}}
Let $\mathcal C$ map moment series (truncated at $K\!\le\!3$) to cumulants arranged by words.
For window laws within the EF ball,
\[
\|\mathcal C(M_1)-\mathcal C(M_2)\| \ \le\ (1+c_{\mathrm{ne}}\varepsilon)\ \|M_1-M_2\|.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[Finite-$K$ factorization stability]\label{cor:fact-stab}
\emph{\layertag{Alg/Stoch}}
If $M_0$ factorizes to order $K$ and $\|M-M_0\|\le\delta$ on the EF ball, then
\(
\|\bm\kappa_{\mathrm{mixed}}(M)\|\le (1+c_{\mathrm{ne}}\varepsilon)\,\delta.
\)
Hence the radius-gated factorization test (Prop.~\ref{prop:factK-ops}) is $\varepsilon$-stable.
\end{corollary}

\emph{\layertag{Rep/Alg}}
Let $\mathrm{in\_tube}$ be the frontier-tube flag and $\mathrm{clamp}\in\{\textsc{Green},\textsc{Yellow},\textsc{Red}\}$.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item If $\mathrm{in\_tube}=\textsf{true}$: disable merges spanning $\Gamma$–$G$; freeze $\Pi$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:calc2}).

\item Always: reject any $(a,b)$ not in $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{adm}}$; log the violation attempt with registry hash.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\section{Computational Statistics and Reporting}\label{sec:stats2}

\noindent\textbf{Scope.}

\subsection*{Estimators and confidence procedures}

\paragraph{HAC CLT for smooth functionals.}
Let $Z_k$ be a stationary $\alpha$-mixing sequence with summable coefficients and let $\theta=\theta(\mathbb E[Z])$ be a smooth functional (e.g., entries of $\bm\kappa$, $M_t$, band-sampled PR margins).
Define the Newey–West estimator with bandwidth $b$:
\[
\widehat{\mathrm{Var}}_{\mathrm{HAC}}(\hat\theta)
=\Gamma_0+\sum_{h=1}^{b}w_h(\Gamma_h+\Gamma_h^\top),
\quad
w_h=1-\frac{h}{b+1},
\]
where $\Gamma_h$ are lag-$h$ sample autocovariances of the score.
Wald CIs use $\mathcal N(0,\widehat{\mathrm{Var}}_{\mathrm{HAC}})$; $b$ is picked by plug-in and logged.

\begin{lemma}[HAC CLT (ops)]\label{lem:hac2}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch/Comp}}
Under $\alpha$-mixing and smoothness, HAC-based Wald CIs are valid for $\theta$; radius gating (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}) ensures Lipschitz score bounds.
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Moving block bootstrap (MBB) for non-smooths.}
For functionals with kinks/counts (e.g., $|E_t|$, $\widehat R_t=\prod_{i\le r}\sigma_i$, spectral gaps),
use block length $b=o(\constref{tau})$ and $B$ resamples.
\begin{proposition}[Block bootstrap validity]\label{prop:bb2}
\emph{\layertag{Stoch/Comp}}
If $b\to\infty$, $b/\constref{tau}\to 0$, the MBB consistently estimates the sampling distribution; percentile or BCa CIs are valid.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Tail risk.}
For any KPI stream $Z$, report $\mathrm{CVaR}_{0.95}(Z)$ via Rockafellar–Uryasev program (Sec.~\ref{sec:closure2}); export $(\tau^\star,\lambda^\star)$.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Quarantine, coverage, and realized error rates}

\paragraph{Window quarantine (selection hygiene).}
If a preconditioned check fails (e.g., envelope $dv/d\rho=\eta^\star$ mismatch; Sec.~\ref{sec:closure2}), mark the window \textsf{quarantined} and \emph{exclude} it from discovery tallies (FDR, $E_t$, cumulant CIs) and adaptive policy training. Safety KPIs (rights-plane, CVaR slacks) still consume quarantined windows. The Provenance Ladder records \texttt{quarantine=true}, \texttt{reason}, and hashes.

\paragraph{Provenance coverage.}
Define $\mathrm{Coverage}=\frac{\#\text{rows with ladder hashes}}{\#\text{ingested rows}}$ per window and a rolling CI. Coverage drops degrade auditability and can bias inference; they are wired to clamp decisions (below).

\paragraph{Realized FDR (null streams).}
Weekly, synthesize null streams (pre-registered seeds) and compute realized FDR for the raw BH/BY layer and the promoted $E_t$ after MTC gating. If $\widehat{\mathrm{FDR}}_{\mathrm{real}}>0.9\cutq$, deterministically tighten:
$q\leftarrow0.9q$, $r\leftarrow \gamma_r r$ (default $\gamma_r=0.8$), and \emph{freeze} promotions for $m$ windows (default $m=3$), logging “Clamp Policy Evidence”.

\subsection*{KPI definitions, targets, and alarms}

\begin{constbox}
\textbf{KPIs (definitions $\to$ targets $\to$ alarms).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Coupling Mass} $M_t=\bm\kappa_t^\top\FIM_t^{-1}\bm\kappa_t$ (HAC CI). \\
Target: median $\downarrow\ge20\%$ over $2\tau^\ast$; \emph{Alarm}: CI upper $>B_{\mathrm{saf}}$ or $\constref{chi}$ spikes with $|E_t|$ growth (conditioning leak).
\item \textbf{Effective Breadth} $|E_t|$ (BY@\,$\cutq$, then MTC). \\
Target: non-increasing on stationary tasks; \emph{Alarm}: any promotion missing MTC or Context Registry entry.
\item \textbf{Decision Range} $\widehat R_t=\prod_{i\le r} \sigma_i$ (ellipsoid proxy, bootstrap CI). \\
Target: monotone $\uparrow$ subject to I$_5$; \emph{Alarm}: $\mathrm{CVaR}_{0.95}$ drop beyond band, or conservativity bound violated (Sec.~\ref{sec:ctrl2}).
\item \textbf{Fisher Drift \& Conditioning} $d_{\mathrm F}(P_{t-\constref{\tau}},P_t)$ \& radius $r=\varepsilon/\widehat{\constref{Lpsi}}$ with leakage $\constref{chi}$. \\
Target: within budgets; \emph{Alarm}: two consecutive budget hits $\Rightarrow$ clamp (Sec.~\ref{sec:entropy2}); EF-quadratic use blocked when \texttt{kl\_quad\_bounds} fails.
\item \textbf{Holonomy / Rotational Mass} (Prop.~\ref{prop:holonomy}). \\
Target: decreasing; \emph{Alarm}: rising with flat $N_{\mathrm{cross}}$ or small-gap \emph{HarmMass} increase (Sec.~\ref{sec:kernels2}).
\item \textbf{Harmonic Rotational Mass} $\displaystyle \mathrm{HarmMass}=\sum_j \frac{\vartheta_j}{\max(\delta_{\mathrm{gap},j},\underline\delta)}$. \\
Target: flat or $\downarrow$; \emph{Alarm}: sustained rise $\Rightarrow$ $k$-escalation or mixed-plane control.
\item \textbf{Frontier Health} $(\constref{\taumin}, C_{\mathrm{salt}}, C_{\mathrm{fr}}, \text{duty}, p_{\mathrm{graze}})$. \\
Target: duty $\le10\%$; \emph{Alarm}: $\hat\nu_{\min}\le0$ (transversality failure).
\item \textbf{Chatter Index} $\mathrm{Chatter}:=\frac{\#\text{off-frontier switches violating dwell}}{\#\text{switches}}$ and dwell drift $\Delta\constref{\taumin}$. \\
Target: $0$ chatter; \emph{Alarm}: any violation $\Rightarrow$ switching-stability action (Sec.~\ref{thm:switch-stab}).
\item \textbf{Rights-Plane Violations} $\#\{i:\,h_i(x)<0\}$ and residual lower bound (one-sided CI). \\
Target: $0$; \emph{Alarm}: any violation or residual lower bound $<0$ $\Rightarrow$ rejecting automaton.
\item \textbf{Provenance Coverage \& Latency} coverage \%, $\mathrm{Lat}_t=t_{\text{hash}}{-}t_{\text{ingest}}$. \\
Target: coverage $\ge99\%$, latency $\le$ one window; \emph{Alarm}: shortfall $\Rightarrow$ clamp-tighten (q,r) and freeze promotions $m$ windows.
\item \textbf{Dual-Gap Trend} $(G_t,\mathrm{bw}_t)$ for DRO (Sec.~\ref{sec:closure2}). \\
Target: $G_t\le 10^{-4}$, $\mathrm{bw}_t\downarrow$; \emph{Alarm}: plateau $>10^{-4}$ for $m$ windows $\Rightarrow$ \textsf{DRO Convergence Alarm}.
\item \textbf{Realized FDR} from null streams (raw \& promoted). \\
Target: $\le 0.9\cutq$; \emph{Alarm}: $>0.9\cutq$ $\Rightarrow$ deterministic tighten and freeze (this section).
\end{enumerate}
\end{constbox}

\subsection*{$\omega$-Automata evidence (first-class outputs)}
Specifications are compiled to Büchi/Streett automata:
\[
\textsf{G}(M_t\!\le\!B_{\mathrm{saf}}),\quad
\textsf{G}(h_i\!\ge\!\epsilon_{\min}),\quad
\textsf{F}(R_t\!\ge\!R_{\min}),\quad
\textsf{Freeze}\ \textsf{U}\ \textsf{Decoupled}.
\]
Per window we export:
(i) automaton id and type, (ii) run start/end indices, (iii) accepting/rejecting label, (iv) witness set (visited accepting states), (v) \emph{consumed\_evidence} with exact KPI row hashes (timestamp, KPI-hash), (vi) quarantine flags used by the monitor, and (vii) linked artifacts (QP transcript id, GKYP cert id, DRO trace id).

\begin{proposition}[Monitor correctness contract]\label{prop:omega-contract}
\emph{\layertag{Comp}}
Given the logged proposition stream (KPI thresholds, CBF flags, freeze state) and \textsf{quarantine} marks, recomputing the automata produces identical verdicts and runs. Any mismatch or missing row hash is a hard failure.
\end{proposition}

\section{Conclusions \& Deployment Notes}\label{sec:conclusion2}
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Summary.}
Part~II delivers a \emph{proof-carrying governor}: geometry is \emph{spent} via saltation inside ISS with a per-crossing \emph{Frontier Spend Ledger} (\S\ref{sec:calc2}); identifiability is \emph{gated} by instruments + \emph{IV badges} and MTC with power (\S\ref{sec:stoch2}); robustness is \emph{witnessed} by KKT traces with unique $\eta^\star$ and envelope checks (\S\ref{sec:closure2}); EF radii are \emph{enforced} by deterministic leakage clamps and a window \emph{quarantine} policy (\S\ref{sec:entropy2}, \S\ref{sec:stats2}); control claims are \emph{certified} by GKYP/PR over uncertainty with a vertex/extremal \emph{hitmap} and an automatic \emph{band-retuning} rule (\S\ref{sec:ctrl2}); algebra is \emph{registry-bound} with an associator budget (\S\ref{sec:adv-structures2}); and reporting is \emph{deterministically replayable} with coverage KPIs, automata evidence (row-hashes), and realized-FDR tightening (\S\ref{sec:stats2}). Plane drift is bounded and acted upon via holonomy KPIs, the small-gap \emph{Harmonic Rotational Mass} panel, and a gap-calibrated degeneracy policy (\S\ref{sec:kernels2}).

\subsection*{Rollback \& fail-closed policy}
Any of the following triggers immediate \emph{freeze+dwell}, promotion freeze, EF-radius tighten, and safe-bandit fallback to a previously verified lever (\S\ref{sec:ctrl2}):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item rights-plane violation or one-sided residual lower bound $<0$ (CBF); 
\item \textsf{Conservativity Violation} (residual CI lower $>0$);
\item clamp \textsc{Red} persisting $>m$ windows or \emph{coverage} $<99\%$ for $m$ windows (\S\ref{sec:stats2});
\item realized-FDR $>0.9\,\cutq$ (weekly) or missing IV badges on promotions;
\item DK-bound violation or spectral-gap CI containing $0$ without $k$-escalation/mixed-plane engagement;
\item PR sampling validation shows minimum sampled margin $<\constref{epsilon}$ or vertex/extremal \emph{hitmap} shifts beyond tolerance;
\item missing automata \emph{consumed\_evidence} row-hashes or Ladder link.
\end{enumerate}
Automatic actions: (i) shrink $(r,q)$ per posture policy; (ii) raise PR margin target if feasible; (iii) shrink band $[\omega_\ell,\omega_u]$ via the retuning rule; (iv) enforce minimum dwell for controller switching; (v) disable cross-channel merges in \textsc{Red}.

\subsection*{Operational runbook (SRE-facing)}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Daily.}
Verify Ladder coverage $\ge 99\%$ and latency $\le$ one window; inspect \emph{Frontier Spend Ledger} and Frontier Health (duty-cycle, $p_{\mathrm{graze}}$, $\hat\nu_{\min}$ CIs); confirm PR sampling validation vs.\ certificate band and update \emph{hitmap}; re-monitor last 24h automata with \emph{consumed\_evidence} hashes; check quarantine hygiene and risk-posture state.
\item \textbf{Weekly.}
Run realized-FDR audit (synthetic null); deterministically tighten $(\cutq,r)$ and freeze promotions $m$ windows if $>0.9\cutq$; rebuild Replay Pack image and record SHA; review dual-gap trend and $\eta^\star$ bracket; evaluate Harmonic Rotational Mass and holonomy/N$_{\mathrm{cross}}$; review safe-bandit logs (gain/cost, CVaR forecasts, zero-violation constraint).
\item \textbf{On clamp \textsc{Yellow/Red}.}
Shrink $(r,\cutq)$ per policy; disable cross-channel merges in \textsc{Red}; switch sensing to HSIC/CVaR-only triggers; freeze promotions and log posture transition.
\item \textbf{When degeneracy detected.}
If CI$(\Delta\sigma)$ contains $0$, escalate plane dimension $k$ or engage mixed-plane controller; attempt common-$V$; else enforce minimum dwell (record $\tau_{\mathrm{dwell}}$).
\item \textbf{If PR infeasible or margins crowd $\constref{epsilon}$.}
Activate band-retuning (shrink $[\omega_\ell,\omega_u]$ per rule); inspect uncertainty \emph{hitmap} for dominating vertices/extremals; if necessary, raise PR margin and reduce admissible set until sampling validation passes.
\item \textbf{If coverage drops $<99\%$.}
Pause promotions; re-run ingestion audit; tighten $(r,q)$; backfill hashes; resume only after coverage $\ge 99\%$ for $m$ consecutive windows.
\end{enumerate}
