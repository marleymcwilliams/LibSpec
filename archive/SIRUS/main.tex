\begin{GoldenRuleBlock}
\GoldenRuleBody{
\textbf{Purpose.} This work proposes governance and control rails intended to \emph{reduce harm} while enabling safe progress. Apply a \textbf{Golden-Rule framing}: for any proposed action, first check the mirrored-risk bound
\[
\mathrm{risk}_{\text{in}\leftarrow\text{out}} \le \rho\,\mathrm{risk}_{\text{out}\leftarrow\text{in}},\qquad \rho\in(0,1].
\]
If this bound fails, the move is \textsc{MUST--FAIL} (\texttt{GOLDEN\_RULE\_BREACH}) and admission to Q* is disallowed.
}
\end{GoldenRuleBlock}

\begin{SafetyBlock}

If distress arises, pause and step down to the gentle path; defer implementation sections until stable.
}
\end{SafetyBlock}

\begin{EthicBlock}

}

\section*{Author's Note}

This manuscript grows from a commitment to \textbf{harm reduction, transparency, and alignment with the Golden Rule}.  
Our aim is not only to explore technical mechanisms for governance and control, but also to ensure that such work remains safe, interpretable, and compassionate to its readers.  

We recognize that complex formalism can provoke discomfort or dissonance; accordingly, we place ethics, reader care, and stability first. The technical material is scaffolded by clear fail-safe conditions and normative rails so that the pursuit of solvability does not outpace the protection of people.  

In this spirit, the following sections should be read as both a proposal and an invitation: to engage carefully, to question openly, and to help refine the scaffolding of trustworthy progress.

\section{GSE\texorpdfstring{$\leftrightarrow$}{↔}, CDG, Mirror, Sentinel}
\subsection*{GSE (bilateral)}

\subsection*{CDG (evidence-gated coupling)}
Mixed cumulants, permutation/phase-shuffle tests, optional MI bounds. Grades: \texttt{MECH\_EXACT | MECH\_RELAXED | UNSET | COINCIDENTAL}. Admit Q* only on \texttt{EXACT} or priced \texttt{RELAXED}.

\subsection*{Mirror (symmetry \& Golden Rule)}
Enforce symmetry bands and the mirrored-risk bound prior to any admission to Q*.

\subsection*{Sentinel (vigilance)}
Continuous drift/anomaly/adversary checks; any veto defers or reverts to GSE\texorpdfstring{$\leftrightarrow$}{↔}.
