\section*{Tenets and Contracts}

\begin{standbox}[Standing Hypotheses (H1--H6)]
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,label=(H\arabic*)]
  \item Decision space $\Gamma$ is smooth/connected; symmetry by Lie group when needed.
  \item $G$ is Whitney-stratified; dynamics specified per stratum and prolonged (Filippov) at frontiers.
  \item Classical identities hold stratumwise; frontier crossings induce a defect budget per crossing.
  \item Evaluation functionals are Borel and $L^2$ under invariant/windowed laws.
  \item EF neighborhood at independence with Fisher $\constref{F}\!\succ\!0$ and Hessian-Lipschitz $\constref{Lpsi}$.
  \item Controllers are Dirac-projected and band-limited PR on $[\omega_L,\omega_H]$ with margin $\constref{epsilon}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{standbox}
