# (a) Geometric diagnosis — DEC/Hodge split, dwell, holonomy, Fisher continuity (revised)

**Goal:** Preserve I₁–I₅ with explicit, auditable margins; guarantee **persistent feasibility** of the safety kernel; harden interfaces at hybrid frontiers and data/model boundaries.

## A1. Invariants & operating bounds (hard gates)

- **I₁ No-Zeno dwell at frontiers (hybrid safety)**
    
    - Compute **τ_min** via CI on normal speed `ν̂_min`, saltation bound `C_salt`, add safety factor `γ_dwell ≥ 1.5`.
        
    - Enforce **Average Dwell Time (ADT)** supervisor with budgeted switch rate; **frontier freeze** of projector Π during crossings.
        
    - **Acceptance checks (replay):** (i) tube residuals `LB_i ≥ 0`, (ii) `‖K − I‖_obs ≤ C_salt(1+0.1)`, (iii) ADT margin ≥ 0.
        
- **I₂ Statistical locality & provenance continuity (two-channel)**
    
    - EF radius `r = ε_EF / L_ψ` (kl-quad sandwich bounds) **and** OOB shift (`HSIC_p`, `MMD_p`) must both pass.
        
    - Continuity targets: ≤5% MAPE up to k=4, **CVaR₀.₉₅** preserved. Failing either ⇒ `Clamp=Red`.
        
- **I₃ Identifiability-first discovery (correlation ≠ coupling)**
    
    - Promotions require **pre-registered tests** (HSIC + FDR) **and** instrument/lag sanity.
        
    - Triggers: ρ≥0.6 or Kendall-τ≥0.5 **or** HSIC p<0.01 (post-FDR). **Power ≥ 0.8** documented.
        
- **I₄ Actuation-basis stability (holonomy/DEC + degeneracy sentinels)**
    
    - Track **Holonomy** and **Harmonic Rotational Mass** as **budgets**; add **λ_gap_min** and **κ(A)** sentinels.
        
    - Two consecutive sentinel hits ⇒ basis freeze + escalate `k` before updates.
        
- **I₅ Rights-plane & PR stability (CBF/CLF-QP with robust PR)**
    
    - Runtime **CBF/CLF-QP** enforces barrier invariance; **finite-band PR** witness via **GKYP** over Ω; band re-tunes if margin crowds ε.
        
    - **Default-deny**: missing/invalid telemetry or expired Ω ⇒ lock.
        

> **Meta-guard (Anti-Goodhart):** treat **coupling mass** `M_t = κ_tᵀ F_t^{-1} κ_t` as a **diagnostic only**. If `M_t` drops without corroborating process change or test power, set `Clamp=Amber` and require operator review.

## A2. DEC/Hodge operationalization (interfaces you can actually run)

- Maintain a DEC complex per mode; compute per-window estimates of **gradient/solenoidal/harmonic** energy; **harmonic mass** trends feed the degeneracy sentinel.
    
- Holonomy budget = line integral of connection around closed loops (estimated from control-space cycles); budget breach ⇒ **basis freeze** + dwell escalation.
    

## A3. Anti-normalization-of-deviance

- Any threshold relaxation (dwell, EF radius, q, grazing `p_thr`, Ω bounds) requires: (i) risk memo + sims, (ii) **dual approval** (safety champion + domain lead), (iii) **reversion ≤ 21 days**, (iv) **ThresholdChange VC** (m-of-n).

# (b) Safety kernel — **optimal-decay CLF-CBF-QP** (always-feasible) with ω-automata/SPRT wiring

**Mission:** Guarantee **point-wise feasibility** inside `h(x)>0`, isolate the kernel, and wire proofs to Tier-1.

## B1. Optimal-decay safety QP (drop-in)

- Solve at each step:
    
    - **Decision:** `u, δ, ω`
        
    - **Minimize:** `½ uᵀH u + Fᵀu + pδ² + q(ω−ω₀)²`
        
    - **s.t.**
        
        - **CBF:** `L_f h + L_g h ⋅ u ≥ − ω α(h)` (ω ≥ 0, relaxed by cost)
            
        - **CLF:** `L_f V + L_g V ⋅ u ≤ −γ V + δ`
            
        - **Actuator:** `u ∈ U_adm`
            
- **Feasibility guarantee:** For any `h(x)>0` and convex `U_adm`, there exists `(u, ω)` s.t. CBF is satisfiable; ω grows to ensure feasibility (penalized).
    

> **Export (Tier-1):** `ω*`, `δ*`, CBF slack CVaR witness, QP KKT residual norm.

## B2. Safety kernel isolation & interface

- Safety kernel runs in **protected process**, receives nominal `u_nom`, returns `u_safe`.
    
- If QP solver fails or `h(x)≤0`, **fail-safe** motion (`u=π_brake`) + **lock**; raise `A_PROOF_DRIFT_HARD`.
    

## B3. DRO envelope precondition + **SPRT** on coupling mass

- Before any SPRT on `M_t`, verify **envelope identity** `d/dρ E[φ_η] |_{η=η⋆} ≈ η⋆` within tolerance; else **quarantine** (`Clamp=Red`).
    
- Register `{H₀: M_t ≤ B_saf, H₁: M_t ≥ B_risk, α, β, δ, boundaries, stop_time, verdict}`. Link transcript id in **CIS** and **Tier-1**.
    

## B4. ω-automata (accept/reject)

- `A_STATE_RETRY_BUDGET` (CAS liveness), `A_OOB_SHIFT_DIVERGENCE`, `A_PROOF_DRIFT_HARD`, `A_VC_CHAIN_INCOMPLETE`, `A_BACKPRESSURE_FAILSAFE`, `A_ML_FAIRNESS_GATE`, `A_THRESHOLD_RELAXATION_GUARD`.
    
- Temporal logic (LTL):
    
    - `G(¬fresh(backpressure) → X locked)`
        
    - `G(valid(Ω) ∧ PR_ok → allow) ∧ G(¬valid(Ω) → ¬allow)`
        
    - `G((EF_ok ∧ OOB_ok) ∨ quarantine)`
        
    - `G(λ_gap_min < λ_floor ∨ κ>κ_max → X freeze_basis)`
